######## row26_order : is p+-first safe? ########
==============================================================================
1.  THE p+ MASTERS AT FIXED z ARE NOT LOGARITHMS -- THEY ARE Ei's
==============================================================================
   at fixed z the p+ dependence is  (rational in p+) x exp(-i alpha p+) ,
      alpha = k.(y'-z)/k+   comes from the phase e^{-i (p+/k+) k.(y'-z)} .
   so every master is an exponential integral, e.g.
      int dp+ e^{-i a p+}/p+            = -Ei(-i a p+)          (or E_1)
      int dp+ e^{-i a p+}/(p+ A + k+ B) = (1/A) e^{i a k+B/A} Ei(-i a (p+ + k+B/A))
      numeric (0.06675785052 - 0.1384686968j)   closed (0.06675785052 - 0.1384686968j)   diff 2.3e-26
   -> doing p+ first turns every term into Ei's of a z-dependent argument, and you
      still have to do the five transverse integrals afterwards.

==============================================================================
2.  AND THE p+-INTEGRATED RESULT HAS A 1/|z|^2 TAIL
==============================================================================
   <J(z)> x |z|^2 , with Lambda = 1e-6 , p+ up to 5 :
   |z|             <J> x |z|^2  ratio to previous
   30               -0.0375515                  -
   60               -0.0360262              0.959
   120              -0.0338726              0.940
   240              -0.0299233              0.883
   -> does NOT fall: a flat (indeed slowly growing) |z|^-2 tail, so the z integral
      done AFTER p+ is logarithmically divergent and needs a transverse cutoff R.

   and that cutoff is not free -- it is tied to Lambda:
   Lambda     <J> x |z|^2 at |z|=120
   1e-04              -0.0174456
   1e-06              -0.0338726
   1e-08              -0.0503041
   the tail grows with log(1/Lambda): the transverse log and the rapidity log are
   the SAME log seen from two sides.  Cutting z at R and p+ at Lambda independently
   double counts unless you impose R ~ k+/(Lambda |k_perp|).
