3.  AFTER SUBTRACTION, ARE THE p+ ENDPOINT RESIDUES UV FINITE?
==============================================================================
   Only T2 and T4 have p+ poles, and they are also two of the three UV-divergent blocks.
   So the two divergences sit on the SAME blocks and the order matters.  Subtract first:
       counterterm(T1) = eta etabar  x W e^{i etabar k.(z-x)}/(z-x)^2 / k+
       counterterm(T2) = eta/etabar  x  "    "
       counterterm(T4) = etabar/eta  x  "    "
   then take the endpoint residue.  Numerically, u^2 x <residue> as u = z-x -> 0 :
