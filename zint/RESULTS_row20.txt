==============================================================================
A.  THE z-SIDE DECIDES WHETHER YOU GET N_c AT ALL
==============================================================================
   both U(z)          (as your six parts have it) :  ||C22 - N_c U(x')U(x)|| =  9.326e-15   COLLAPSES
   both U-dagger(z)                               :  ||C22 - N_c U(x')U(x)|| =  5.329e-15   COLLAPSES
   MIXED: bra U(z), ket U-dagger(z)  (top line)   :  ||C22 - N_c U(x')U(x)|| =  2.584e+00   DOES NOT COLLAPSE

   sum_b U^{bc'}U^{bc} = delta^{c'c}  and  sum_b U^{c'b}U^{cb} = delta^{c'c},
   but  sum_b U^{bc'}U^{cb} = [U U]^{cc'}  is NOT a delta.  So the collapse needs
   the SAME index order on both sides -- either both U(z) or both U^dag(z).
   Your top line writes  U^{dag bc}(z)  in the ket and  U^{bc'}(z)  in the bra;
   your expanded parts silently use U^{bc}(z) for both.  Make the top line match.
