7.  WHICH OF THE THREE LOGS NEEDS A REGULATOR
==============================================================================
   z is the UNMEASURED GLUON's transverse position: it carries U(z) and is integrated
     over all space.  Both of its logs are real:
       z -> x        ULTRAVIOLET , residue C_UV(eta) = P_gg(eta)/(2 N_c) , d_perp-free
       |z| -> oo     INFRARED    , residue (1/d_perp)(k+-p+)/p+ + k+/(k+-p+)
                                   -- must cancel against the Bbar_2 (virtual) term,
                                      where U(z) -> 1 : the same BK mechanism as Row IV.

   y and x' are SOURCE positions: they sit under rho^f(y) and rho^{b'}(x') .
     The 1/y^2 tail is therefore controlled by the target correlator <rho rho>, not by
     a regulator.  It is not a divergence of the loop integral.

   p+ -> 0 and p+ -> k+ are RAPIDITY divergences, cut by Lambda : Int_Lambda^{k+-Lambda}.

   ==> THE UV TERM IS  z -> x , and its coefficient is

       [1/(2pi)^3] (g^4/8 pi^4) (1/k+) Int dp+/2pi  P_gg(p+/k+)/(2 N_c)
          x  (x'-w').(y-x) / [ (x'-w')^2 (y-x)^2 ]  x  Int_UV d^2z / (z-x)^2
          x  (colour + sources) ,

   i.e. the UV residue is  P_gg  times the product of the two LO Weizsacker-Williams
   kernels -- exactly the DGLAP form, and with no d_perp left over.
