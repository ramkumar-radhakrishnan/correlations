============================================================================
   attach the MS-bar factor (e^gamma/4pi)^eps that rides on g^2 mu^{2eps}:
      C(eps)(e^gamma/4pi)^eps (r^2 mu^2)^{-eps}/eps = pi*(epsilon*(2 - log(4*pi**2*mu**2*r2)) + 1)/epsilon

   cutoff dictionary:  (pi/2)(log R^2/r^2 + 1) = (pi/2)[Lcal + 2]  =>  R = e^{1/2}/(2 pi mu)
      solve: [exp(1/2)/(2*pi*mu)]

   (the surviving phase is e^{-ik.r}), so Lcal is the logarithm between the
   factorisation scale mu and the hard transverse scale ~ 1/r ~ k_perp.

STEP 7.  CONTRACT INTO THE ROW, IN THE SPLITTING VARIABLE
============================================================================
   zeta = k+/(p+ + k+) , zetabar = p+/(p+ + k+) :  the C vertex splits ONE gluon
   of S = p+ + k+ into the measured k+ and the unobserved p+.  Then
      a = S/p+ = 1/zetabar ,   b = S/k+ = 1/zeta .
   the two C brackets contract to
      c1 = d_perp - 2/zetabar - 2/zeta   (delta_km delta_k'm')
      c2 = 1/zeta^2 + 1/zetabar^2        (delta_mm' delta_kk')
      c3 = 2/(zeta zetabar)              (delta_km' delta_k'm)

   T^{mm'} IS SYMMETRIC (step 4), so c1 and c3 contract identically and only c1+c3 acts:
      c1 + c3 = d_perp  = d_perp exactly, because
      2/(zeta zetabar) - 2/zeta - 2/zetabar = 2[1 - zeta - zetabar]/(zeta zetabar) = 0
   -> the 2/k+ term of the bracket dies.  It is the momentum sum rule.

   with d_perp = 2-2eps :  d_perp/(2eps) = 1/eps - 1 , (tr T)/Tc = d/(2eps) - 1 = 1/eps - 2,
   and  Tc/eps -> pi(Lcal+2) , Tc -> pi :
