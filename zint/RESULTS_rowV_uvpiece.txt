2. TENSOR vs SCALAR UV TERM (d_perp = d)
==============================================================================
   <rhat^i rhat^m>_d = delta/d, so d_perp xi xib (P.r)(s.r)/r^4 -> (d_perp/d) xi xib (P.s)/r^2 = xi xib (P.s)/r^2
   => the user's scalar xi xib term has the same pole; the traceless difference is UV finite
      and belongs to the rest of 2b (it may be evaluated at d_perp = 2 in d = 2).

6. THE r -> s ARTEFACT AT xi -> 0 AND ITS CANCELLATION
==============================================================================
   full block T4 (the only 1/xi block) at r = s : 0
   user's (xib/xi)(P.s)/(P^2 r^2) at r = s      : -(xi - 1)*(P1*s1 + P2*s2)/(xi*(P1**2 + P2**2)*(s1**2 + s2**2))
   => the user's piece has 1/D ~ 1/((r-s)^2+M^2) with a non-zero residue at r = s:
      Int d^2r near s gives pi e^{iq.s} log(1/M^2) -> pi e^{ik.s} log(1/xi); the full T4 vanishes there.
      The rest of 2b therefore carries -W e^{ik.s} log(1/xi)/xi, and the (1/2) e^{ik.s} L^2 cancels in the sum.
