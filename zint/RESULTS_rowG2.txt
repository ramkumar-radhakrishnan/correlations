2.  THE w , w' INTEGRATION
==============================================================================
   delta^(2)[ y - w + (p+/S)(w-z) ] = delta^(2)[ y - (1-b)w - b z ] ,  b = p+/S
     => w = (y - b z)/(1-b) ,  Jacobian 1/(1-b)^2 = S^2/k+^2   per delta
     => w - z = (y-z)/(1-b)   so   (w-z)^m/(w-z)^2 = (1-b) (y-z)^m/(y-z)^2   per kernel
     => w' - w = (y'-y)/(1-b) = (S/k+)(y'-y)      <-- THE PHASE PICKS UP S/k+
   net from the two deltas and the two kernels : (kplus + pplus)**2/kplus**2  = S^2/k+^2
   so the scalar in front of the 3-term tensor is  (p+k+/S^4)(S^2/k+^2) = pplus/(S**2*kplus)

   YOUR (0.2) has  (1/k+)*(S/k+)*[bracket].  Comparing term by term:
     km,k'm'     yours/mine = (kplus + pplus)/kplus
     mm',kk'     yours/mine = (kplus + pplus)/kplus
     km',mk'     yours/mine = (kplus + pplus)/kplus
   => the relative weights are RIGHT; the overall (k+ + p+)/k+ should not be there.
      correct prefactor for your bracket is simply 1/k+ .
