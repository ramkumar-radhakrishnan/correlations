==============================================================================
WHY THAT MATTERS FOR THE + PRESCRIPTION
==============================================================================
   At FIXED transverse points the identity is exact and harmless:
      g(p+) = e^{-i kappa p+}/(p+ A + k+ B) ,  g(0) = 1/(k+ B)
      int_Lam^P dp/p g = g(0) log(P/Lam) + int_0^P dp/p [g - g(0)]   (exact)

   The trouble is the z integral that follows.  At large |z|:
      A = (x-z)^2 ~ |z|^2  so  g(p+) ~ 1/(p+ |z|^2) -> 0 ,  but  g(0) = 1/(k+B) is
      z-INDEPENDENT.  And kappa ~ |z|, so the phase damps the full integrand only for
      p+ |kappa| |z| >> 1, i.e. only out to  |z|_max ~ k+/(|k| p+).
   The damping scale DEPENDS ON p+ : the z and p+ integrations do not commute.

   |z|_max ~ k+/(|k| p+):
      p+ = 1e-01     ->  |z|_max ~       12.4
      p+ = 1e-02     ->  |z|_max ~      124.0
      p+ = 1e-03     ->  |z|_max ~     1240.3
      p+ = 1e-04     ->  |z|_max ~    12403.5

   consequence: the log(Lambda) multiplies a z-integral that is itself log divergent,
   and log|kappa| ~ log|z| against d^2z/|z|^2 gives a SECOND logarithm.  So after the
   z integration the row carries  log(1/Lambda) x log(R)  and  log^2(R)  --  equivalently
   a MIXED  (1/eps) log(1/Lambda)  once z is regulated in d = 2-2eps.
