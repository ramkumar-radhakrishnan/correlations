   -> a genuine 1/x^2 tail.  At xi = 0 the total phase exp[-ik.(w' - xibar z - xi x)]
      has no x in it, so Int d^2x of this residue diverges logarithmically at large |x|:
      the soft gluon (p+ -> 0) sitting far from the others.  It is the mirror image,
      under (x <-> z, xi <-> xibar), of the xi -> 1 log that shows up inside the r integral.
