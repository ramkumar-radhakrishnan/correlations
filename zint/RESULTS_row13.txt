============================================================================
WHY IT WORKS -- the exact decomposition
============================================================================
   J3 = int dp/[(p+k+)D] = (1/(k+(B-A))) [ log((p+k+)/(pA+k+B)) ]_Lambda^{V-k+}
                         = (1/(k+(B-A))) log[ V(Lambda A+k+B) / ((Lambda+k+)((V-k+)A+k+B)) ]
   so   (A-B)/(2C) J3 = -(1/(2 k+ C)) [ log(V/(Lambda+k+)) - log(((V-k+)A+k+B)/(Lambda A+k+B)) ]
        -> your delta^ij/(2C) in group 1  AND  your group 2.   Exact endpoints kept. 

   J1 = int dp/[p D] = (1/(k+B)) log[ (V-k+) k+B / (Lambda((V-k+)A+k+B)) ]
                     = (1/(k+B)) [ log((V-k+)/Lambda) - log(((V-k+)A+k+B)/(Lambda A+k+B)) ]
                       + O(Lambda)
        -> your group 3 (the blue one) AND the -P^{ij}/B piece of group 1.

   J2 = int dp/D = (1/A) log[((V-k+)A+k+B)/(Lambda A+k+B)]
        -> the +K^{ij}/A piece of group 1.   All five bracket terms accounted for.

============================================================================
BOOKKEEPING OF THE LOGS
============================================================================
   rapidity log(1/Lambda) : ONLY in the blue group-3 term, residue P^{ij}/(k+ B).
                            -> apply [1/p+]_+ to that term alone.
   log V : appears in group 1 (via L1 ~ log(V A/(k+B))) and group 2 (via L2 ~ log(V/k+)).
           For the delta^ij structure the two CANCEL:
              log(V/(Lambda+k+)) - log(((V-k+)A+k+B)/(Lambda A+k+B)) -> log(k+B/(k+ A)) = log(B/A)
           leaving  -delta^ij log[(x-w)^2/(x-z)^2] / (2 k+ (z-w)^2) , free of V and Lambda.
           For K^{ij}/A the log V SURVIVES: residue 1/(x-z)^2 -- a genuine large-p+ log.
      check: log(V/(Lam+k+)) - L1 =   1.15546544   vs  log(B/A) =   1.15546769
      check: log(V/(Lam+k+)) - L1 =  -0.26305006   vs  log(B/A) =  -0.26305032
