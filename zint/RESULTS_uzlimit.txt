B. could it instead follow from UNITARITY of U(z)?  Check the four products.
   (adjoint U is real orthogonal: U^{ab}U^{cb} = U^{ba}U^{bc} = delta^{ac})
   bra1 x ket1 : U^{d'b}(z) U^{bc}(z)      max|M - delta| = 1.466   -> U(z)^2, NOT delta
   bra1 x ket2 : U^{d'b}(z) U^{db}(z)      max|M - delta| = 0.000   -> delta (unitarity)
   bra2 x ket1 : U^{bd'}(z) U^{bc}(z)      max|M - delta| = 0.000   -> delta (unitarity)
   bra2 x ket2 : U^{bd'}(z) U^{db}(z)      max|M - delta| = 1.466   -> U(z)^2, NOT delta

   so only the CROSS terms collapse by unitarity; the diagonal ones give U(z)^2.
   The quoted expression is therefore NOT a unitarity rearrangement --
   it is the U(z) -> 1 limit.
