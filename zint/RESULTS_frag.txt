============================================================================
1.  WHAT IS THE PHASE CONJUGATE TO?
============================================================================
   the two C deltas gave   w - z = (S/k+)(y - z) ,  w' - z = (S/k+)(y' - z),
   hence  w' - w = (S/k+)(y' - y) = (S/k+) r   and
      e^{-ik.(w'-w)} = e^{-i (S/k+) k.r}
   with zeta = k+/S :   S/k+ = 1/zeta , so
      e^{-ik.(w'-w)} = e^{-i (k_perp/zeta).r}
   substitution check  S/k+ with S = k+/zeta : 1/zeta

   => r = y'-y is conjugate to  k_perp/zeta ,  NOT to k_perp.
      k_perp/zeta is the PARENT's transverse momentum: if the splitting is
      collinear the daughter carries zeta of both + and transverse momentum,
      so k_perp^parent = k_perp/zeta.

   THIS IS THE FRAGMENTATION SIGNATURE: the hard factor is evaluated at k/zeta,
   not at k.  A PDF-type (initial-state) collinear log would leave the hard
   factor at the measured k and put the zeta convolution on the incoming flux.

============================================================================
2.  AND THE MEASURE
============================================================================
   we found   dp+ = k+ d zeta / zeta^2   at fixed k+, giving  int d zeta/zeta^2 .
   the standard collinear fragmentation formula for the INVARIANT cross section is
      E dsigma^h/d^3k = sum_i int_x^1 (dz/z^2) D_{h/i}(z) [E' dsigmahat^i/d^3k']_{k'=k/z}
   -- the same dz/z^2.   A PDF convolution instead carries dxi/xi with the parton
   density, not dz/z^2 with the hard factor shifted to k/z.

   so all three kinematic signatures line up with FRAGMENTATION:
      (a) coefficient P_gg(zeta),  zeta = fraction of the PARENT taken by the
          MEASURED gluon;
      (b) measure d zeta/zeta^2 ;
      (c) hard factor at k/zeta (through the phase).

============================================================================
3.  WHERE THE COLLINEAR REGION SITS
============================================================================
   two final-state gluons k (measured) and p (unobserved) are collinear when
      M^2 = (p+ k_perp - k+ p_perp)^2/(p+ k+)  -> 0 ,  i.e. relative q_perp -> 0.
   small relative q_perp  <=>  LARGE relative transverse separation.
   that is exactly where the two-C row diverges (|z| -> infinity), and NOT at any
   collapse of points.  Final-state collinear singularities live at large distance;
   ultraviolet ones live at small distance.  The row has the first and not the second.
