The $p^{+}$–integrated $|\mathfrak B_{2}|^{2}$ row

All six parts $2a$–$2f$, checked against the exact masters

$A=(x-z)^{2}$, $B=(x-w)^{2}$, $C=(z-w)^{2}$, $A'=(x'-z)^{2}$, $B'=(x'-w')^{2}$, $C'=(z-w')^{2}$; $\;\Delta\equiv AB'-A'B$, $\;\alpha=k^{+}B/A$, $\;\alpha'=k^{+}B'/A'$

The integration is correct — all six parts, exactly. Each part's coefficient reproduces its $p^{+}$ master to $10^{-16}$–$10^{-14}$ relative, on four random configurations with $\Lambda$ ranging over $10^{-8}$–$10^{-4}$ and $\vee$ over $10^{4}$–$10^{8}$ (§1). The prefactor $\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{5}}$ now correctly includes the $\frac{1}{2\pi}$ from the $p^{+}$ measure. Two structural facts are worth knowing before you use it: only $2b$ carries $\log\frac{1}{\Lambda}$ and only $2c$ carries $\log\vee$ (§2), and every $\frac{1}{A-B}$ and $\frac{1}{\Delta}$ pole cancels inside its own part, so you can integrate part by part — but not in double precision near those surfaces (§3). One real slip in $2f$ (§4).

4. What to fix

  1. $2f$ lost the $x,x'$ integrations. All four of its pieces read $\int_{z,w,w'}$ while $2a$–$2e$ correctly read $\int_{z,w,w',x,x'}$. Since $A,B,A',B'$ and $\rho(x),\rho(x')$ all appear in $2f$, it must be $\int_{z,w,w',x,x'}$ too.
  2. $d_{\perp}=2$. No UV pole in this row, so nothing can hit an $O(\epsilon)$ piece.
  3. The Wilson–line transpose is still in all six parts. Your bra has $U^{e'd'}(x')$, your ket $U^{de}(x)$; the bra should be $U^{d'e'}(x')$. With that fixed, the $(2)\times(2)$ colour term collapses to $N_{c}\big[U^{\dagger}(x')U(x)\big]^{e'e}$ — the adjoint dipole.
  4. You dropped the $N_{c}$ collapse you had already derived. These six parts carry the colour as an unexpanded product of the two brackets, which is fine and equivalent, but the fourth combination simplifies unconditionally (no limit needed) and will save you work later.

5. Where this stands

This is the exact, complete $p^{+}$ integration of the $(1\rho)\times(1\rho)$ piece. It is not yet a finite answer, for the two reasons already established:

  • $2b$ still carries $\log\frac{1}{\Lambda}$, which has to be subtracted into the small–$x$ evolution of the target rather than evaluated.
  • The $z$ integral is still log–divergent at $|z|\to\infty$, with coefficient $\frac{1}{4k^{+}}\log\frac{\vee-k^{+}}{\Lambda}\frac{(x-w)\cdot(x'-w')}{(x-w)^{2}(x'-w')^{2}}$ — which, in this decomposition, is what $2b$ and $2c$ produce together (their $\log|z|^{2}$ pieces cancel, and the $\frac{B+B'}{B'-B}\log\frac{B'}{B}$ leftovers cancel against $2a$). That log closes only against $N_{1}$, the $(2\rho)\times(2\rho)$ piece and the virtual rows.
