2. Colour

$\mathfrak B^{bc}_{2}$'s one–$\rho$ piece carries $f^{cba}\rho^{a}(x)$ (free $b,c$). Relabel $a\to d$:

$$U^{ac}(w)\,\mathfrak B^{bc(1)}\ \to\ f^{cbd}\,U^{ac}(w)\,\rho^{d}(x),\qquad -U^{\dagger bc}(z)\,\bar{\mathfrak B}^{ca(1)}\ \to\ -f^{acd}\,U^{bc}(z)\,U^{de}(x)\,\rho^{e}(x).$$
$f$ is not an overall factor here, and you correctly left it inside. The two terms carry $f^{cbd}$ and $f^{acd}$ — different tensors ($\|f^{abc}-f^{bac}\|=9.798$). This is the opposite situation from the $\mathfrak B_{3}$ row, where $f$ could be pulled out, and from the $\mathfrak A^{(3)}$ row, where it was pulled out but should not have been. All three are handled correctly in what you have written here.

The two–$\rho$ piece carries $\{\rho^{c}(y),\rho^{b}(x)\}$; with the relabelling $y\to x'$ (the $i$–slot, paired with $w'$) and $x\to y$ (the $j$–slot, paired with $z$) this becomes $\{\rho^{c'}(x'),\rho^{b}(y)\}$ — your bracket. $\checkmark$

One transpose. The row has $U^{c'a}(w')$; you wrote $U^{ac'}(w')$. Same as in the last three rows — pick one ordering and use it in both terms of the bracket, otherwise the difference is not a well–defined matrix difference.

The two cross terms are each other's mirror, as they should be: in the first the one–$\rho$ vertex is in the ket (structures $(x-w)$, $(x-z)$, $(z-w)$, denominator $D$) and the two–$\rho$ in the bra; in the second they swap, giving $(x-w')$, $(z-w')$ and kernel $\frac{(x'-w)^{i}(y-z)^{j}}{(x'-w)^{2}(y-z)^{2}}$. Both as written. $\checkmark$

3. Where the transverse divergence is

3.1 Not in the ultraviolet

Every subset of $\{x,y,z,w,x',w'\}$ of size $\ge2$ collapsed at scale $\rho$ against the measure $\rho^{2(n-1)}\frac{d\rho}{\rho}$ — all 57 regions converge. Checked twice: on the integrand as written, and on the $p^{+}$–integrated form of §4, where the logarithms $\log\frac{k^{+}B}{\Lambda A}$ and $\log\frac{\vee A}{k^{+}B}$ could in principle have created new short–distance behaviour. They do not: near $x\to w$ or $x\to z$ the logarithm goes as $\log\rho$ while the accompanying tensor supplies at most $\frac{1}{\rho}$, so the integrand is $\frac{\log\rho}{\rho}$ against $\rho\,d\rho$.

3.3 The mechanism and the exact coefficient

At large $|z|$ the one–$\rho$ vertex degenerates — the $\frac{S}{k^{+}}$ structure grows like $|z|$ while $D\sim p^{+}|z|^{2}$ — and the whole bracket collapses onto a product of two Weizsäcker–Williams kernels:

$$\frac{1}{D}\Big[\tfrac{\delta^{ij}(A-B)}{2SC}+\tfrac{\mathcal P^{ij}}{p^{+}}+\tfrac{\mathcal K^{ij}}{k^{+}}\Big] \ \xrightarrow[\;|z|\to\infty\;]{}\ -\frac{1}{2p^{+}k^{+}}\,\frac{(x-w)^{i}}{(x-w)^{2}}\,\frac{(x-z)^{j}}{(x-z)^{2}} .$$

4. The $p^{+}$ integral

All the $p^{+}$ dependence is $\frac{1}{D}$ times the three bracket weights $\frac{1}{2S}$, $\frac{1}{p^{+}}$, $\frac{1}{k^{+}}$, since

$$\mathcal P^{ij}=\frac{(x-w)^{i}(z-w)^{j}}{C}+\frac{(x-w)^{i}(x-z)^{j}}{2A},\qquad \mathcal K^{ij}=\frac{(z-w)^{i}(x-z)^{j}}{C}-\frac{(x-w)^{i}(x-z)^{j}}{2B}$$

are both $p^{+}$–independent. Note $D=p^{+}A+k^{+}B$ has both signs positive, so there is no zero on the contour — this row integrates cleanly, unlike the $\mathfrak B_{3}$ row. Three masters, verified against quadrature at $A=0.3592$, $B=2.0885$, $\Lambda=10^{-8}$, $\vee=10^{7}$:
