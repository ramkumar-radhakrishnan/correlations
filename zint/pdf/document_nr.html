The two–piece $\mathfrak B_{3}$ row

Checking the $p^{+}$ integration term by term

$A\equiv(x-z)^{2}$,  $B\equiv(x-w)^{2}$,  $\beta\equiv\dfrac{k^{+}B}{A}$,  units below: the common $\dfrac{ig^{4}f^{abd}}{16\pi^{5}}=\dfrac{ig^{4}f^{abd}}{8\pi^{4}}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2\pi}$

The algebra is correct. Both prefactors reproduce with ratio exactly $1$, and all three $p^{+}$ integrals agree with principal–value quadrature to $10^{-8}$–$10^{-12}$ across four configurations (§2). Every logarithm combines the way you wrote it — in particular your blue $\big[\log\frac{\vee}{\Lambda}+\log(1-\frac{k^{+}}{\vee})\big]$ is exactly $\log\frac{\vee-k^{+}}{\Lambda}$, and $\big[\log\frac{\vee}{k^{+}-\Lambda}-\log\frac{\vee}{\Lambda}\big]$ is exactly $-\log\frac{k^{+}-\Lambda}{\Lambda}$ (§3). But the result is incomplete in one physical respect: it is the principal value, and the on–shell $i\pi$ piece is missing (§5) — which matters here because every prefactor carries an explicit $i$, so that piece is real. And the $\frac{1}{\Lambda}$ is a power divergence with nothing left to cancel it now that piece II is gone (§4).

3. Why the logarithms combine the way you wrote them

The $\log$ part of $F_{\rm I}$ on $[k^{+}+\Lambda,\vee]$ is

$$\frac{A+B}{k^{+}(A-B)^{2}}\left[\log\frac{|\vee A-k^{+}B|}{|(k^{+}+\Lambda)A-k^{+}B|}-\log\frac{\vee-k^{+}}{\Lambda}\right],$$

and your two terms supply exactly that, because

$$-\log\frac{\vee}{\Lambda}-\log\Big(1-\frac{k^{+}}{\vee}\Big)=-\log\frac{\vee}{\Lambda}-\log\frac{\vee-k^{+}}{\vee}=-\log\frac{\vee-k^{+}}{\Lambda}\quad\checkmark$$

Similarly on $[\Lambda,k^{+}-\Lambda]$ the primitive gives $\log\frac{k^{+}-\Lambda}{\Lambda}+\log\frac{|(k^{+}-\Lambda)A-k^{+}B|}{|\Lambda A-k^{+}B|}$, and your second blue bracket is

$$-\left[\log\frac{\vee}{k^{+}-\Lambda}-\log\frac{\vee}{\Lambda}\right]=\log\frac{k^{+}-\Lambda}{\Lambda}\quad\checkmark$$

— the $\vee$ cancels inside it, as it should. And piece III gives $\frac{1}{k^{+}}\big[-\log\frac{\vee}{\Lambda}+2\log\big|\frac{\vee A-k^{+}B}{\Lambda A-k^{+}B}\big|\big]$, matching your last two terms once you note $\frac{k^{+}B-\vee A}{k^{+}B-\Lambda A}=\frac{\vee A-k^{+}B}{\Lambda A-k^{+}B}$.

In the three–piece version of $\mathfrak B_{3}$ the two halves of piece II had equal and opposite double poles at $p^{+}=k^{+}$ and cancelled each other, but piece I's was still uncancelled. Here, with piece II absent altogether, there is nothing to cancel it at all. And $\frac{1}{\Lambda}$ is not a rapidity logarithm: evolution cannot absorb it. Either it cancels against a companion row, or the $\Theta$ regularisation of the $p^{+}=k^{+}$ pole needs rethinking.

5. What the result is missing: the on–shell piece

$p^{+}A-k^{+}B$ vanishes at $\beta=k^{+}\frac{(x-w)^{2}}{(x-z)^{2}}$, and for generic transverse points that sits inside the integration range:

So the integral is only defined once you supply a prescription. It is a light–cone energy denominator, so the propagator's $+i\epsilon$ gives

$$\frac{1}{p^{+}A-k^{+}B\pm i\epsilon}=\mathrm{PV}\frac{1}{p^{+}A-k^{+}B}\mp i\pi\,\delta\big(p^{+}A-k^{+}B\big).$$
What you wrote is the principal value. The missing $\delta$ pieces are, in the same units, $$\text{pieces I}:\ \mp\,i\pi\,\frac{A+B}{k^{+}(A-B)^{2}}\quad\text{(whenever $\beta$ lies in that range)}, \qquad \text{piece III}:\ \pm\,\frac{2i\pi}{k^{+}} .$$

Note the first is the same coefficient that multiplies the logarithm — as it must be, since both come from the residue at $p^{+}=\beta$.

These are not optional. Every prefactor in this row carries an explicit $i$, so $i\times(\mp i\pi)$ is real and contributes directly to the cross section. Physically it is the on–shell intermediate state — a genuine real–emission contribution. Dropping it loses a real part of the answer.

5.1 A related bookkeeping point

Because $\beta$ can lie on either side, the arguments of your logarithms change sign. For $A=0.62$, $B=1.45$, $\Lambda=10^{-5}$:

$$(\Lambda+k^{+})A-k^{+}B=-0.8300,\qquad \vee A-k^{+}B=+35.7500,$$

so the ratio inside $\log\big[\frac{\vee A-k^{+}B}{(\Lambda+k^{+})A-k^{+}B}\big]$ is negative. The expressions only make sense as $\log|\cdot|$ — which is exactly what the principal value produces, and what the numerical agreement in §2 confirms. Write the absolute–value bars explicitly, so the branch is not left to chance when the imaginary parts are restored.
