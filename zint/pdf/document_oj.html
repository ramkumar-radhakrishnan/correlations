Row IV: the plus prescription, step by step

From the three-block form to evolution + DGLAP + finite, every substitution written out

$S=k^{+}\!+p^{+}$,  $\xi=\dfrac{k^{+}}{S}$,  $\bar\xi=1-\xi=\dfrac{p^{+}}{S}$,  $\xi_{0}=\dfrac{k^{+}}{\vee}$,  $r=y'-y$,  $\kappa=k_{\perp}\!\cdot\!r$,  $L_{r}=\log\big(4\pi^{2}\mu^{2}r^{2}\big)$,  $\dfrac{1}{\bar\epsilon}=\dfrac1\epsilon-\gamma_{E}+\log4\pi$

Your five-term expression is correct. It is checked below against the three-block starting point at three kinematic points; the only residual is $O(\Lambda)$, from extending the non-singular integrals from $\Lambda$ down to $0$, and it scales linearly in $\Lambda$. One scheme point to be aware of: doing $\big[\frac{1}{p^{+}}\big]_{+}$ in $p^{+}$ puts $\log\frac{\vee-k^{+}}{\Lambda}$ on the $\delta$ term, while doing it in $\xi$ puts $\log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}$ there. Both are right; they differ by a finite reshuffling into the $[\ ]_{+}$ remainder. §4 and §5.

1. The starting point

Abbreviate

$$\mathcal X=\frac{(x-y)\!\cdot\!(x'-y')}{(x-y)^{2}(x'-y')^{2}},\qquad \mathcal R=\frac{(x-y)\!\cdot\!(y'\!-\!y)\;(x'-y')\!\cdot\!(y'\!-\!y)}{(x-y)^{2}(x'-y')^{2}(y'-y)^{2}},$$ $$\mathcal M=\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}+1-L_{r},\qquad \mathcal U=\big[U^{e'c}(y')-U^{e'c}(x')\big]\big[U^{ec}(y)-U^{ec}(x)\big]\rho^{e'}(x')\rho^{e}(x),\qquad \mathcal P=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{4\pi^{3}}\frac{N_{c}}{k^{+}}$$

The verified three-block form, with the phase written as $e^{-ik\cdot r}e^{-ik\cdot r\,p^{+}/k^{+}}=e^{-i\kappa S/k^{+}}$, is

$$\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}=\mathcal P\!\int_{\Lambda}^{\vee-k^{+}}\!\frac{dp^{+}}{2\pi}\; e^{-i\kappa S/k^{+}}\!\!\int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!\Bigg\{ \underbrace{\Big[\tfrac{p^{+}}{S^{2}}+\tfrac{p^{+}}{k^{+2}}+\tfrac{1}{p^{+}}\Big]\mathcal M\,\mathcal X}_{\rm(1)} \;\underbrace{-\;d_{\perp}\tfrac{p^{+}}{S^{2}}\,\mathcal R}_{\rm(2)} \;\underbrace{-\Big[\tfrac{p^{+}}{k^{+2}}+\tfrac{1}{p^{+}}\Big]\mathcal X}_{\rm(3)}\Bigg\}\,\mathcal U$$

2. The plus prescription in $p^{+}$

Write $T=\vee-k^{+}$. The only singularity is $\frac{1}{p^{+}}$ at $p^{+}\to0$, and everything multiplying it depends on $p^{+}$ only through the phase:

$$F(p^{+})=e^{-i\kappa S/k^{+}}=e^{-i\kappa}\,e^{-i\kappa p^{+}/k^{+}},\qquad F(0)=e^{-i\kappa}\ \text{ finite.}$$

That is the whole condition, so

$$\int_{\Lambda}^{T}\!\frac{dp^{+}}{p^{+}}F(p^{+}) =\underbrace{F(0)\log\frac{T}{\Lambda}}_{\delta(p^{+})\ \text{piece}} +\underbrace{\int_{0}^{T}\!dp^{+}\,\frac{F(p^{+})-F(0)}{p^{+}}}_{\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}\!dp^{+}\Big[\frac{1}{p^{+}}\Big]_{+}F}$$ $$\Longleftrightarrow\qquad \frac{\theta(p^{+}-\Lambda)}{p^{+}}=\bigg[\frac{1}{p^{+}}\bigg]_{+}+\delta(p^{+})\,\log\frac{\vee-k^{+}}{\Lambda}+O(\Lambda)$$

Applying it term by term

Block (1) contains one $\frac{1}{p^{+}}$, with companion $F\mathcal M\mathcal X$:

$$\mathcal P\!\int_{0}^{T}\!\frac{dp^{+}}{2\pi}F\Big[\tfrac{p^{+}}{S^{2}}+\tfrac{p^{+}}{k^{+2}}+\big[\tfrac{1}{p^{+}}\big]_{+}\Big]\mathcal M\mathcal X \;+\;\mathcal P\frac{1}{2\pi}e^{-i\kappa}\log\frac{\vee-k^{+}}{\Lambda}\,\mathcal M\,\mathcal X$$

and $\mathcal P\cdot\frac{1}{2\pi}=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{4}}\frac{N_{c}}{k^{+}}$ — your first and second blocks.

Block (2) has no pole ($\propto p^{+}$), so nothing happens; keep $\int_{\Lambda}$ (exact) or extend to $0$ at the cost of $O(\Lambda)$ — your third block.

Block (3) contains the same $\frac{1}{p^{+}}$ with companion $-F\mathcal X$:

$$-\mathcal P\!\int_{0}^{T}\!\frac{dp^{+}}{2\pi}F\Big[\tfrac{p^{+}}{k^{+2}}+\big[\tfrac{1}{p^{+}}\big]_{+}\Big]\mathcal X \;-\;\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{4}}\frac{N_{c}}{k^{+}}e^{-i\kappa}\log\frac{\vee-k^{+}}{\Lambda}\,\mathcal X$$

— your fourth and fifth blocks. Every prefactor and sign matches.

3. The two $\delta$ terms combine

Your second and fifth blocks have the same prefactor, the same log, the same phase $e^{-ik\cdot r}$ and the same $\mathcal X$; only the bracket differs ($\mathcal M$ versus $1$). So

$$\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{4}}\frac{N_{c}}{k^{+}}\log\frac{\vee-k^{+}}{\Lambda} \int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!e^{-ik_{\perp}\cdot r}\,\big[\mathcal M-1\big]\,\mathcal X\,\mathcal U, \qquad \mathcal M-1=\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}-L_{r}$$

and that bracket is precisely $\frac{1}{\pi}\,\delta_{mm'}\mathcal G^{mm'}$, i.e. the $z$ integral put back:

$$\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}-\log\big(4\pi^{2}\mu^{2}r^{2}\big)=\frac{1}{\pi}\int d^{2}z\; \frac{(y-z)\!\cdot\!(y'-z)}{(y-z)^{2}(y'-z)^{2}}$$

This is the real–emission piece of the BK/JIMWLK kernel. Keeping the two blocks separate hides that.

4. Which logarithm? A scheme point, not an error

With $\bar\xi=\frac{p^{+}}{k^{+}+p^{+}}$,

$$\log\bar\xi=\log p^{+}-\log(k^{+}+p^{+})\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{d\bar\xi}{\bar\xi}=\frac{dp^{+}}{p^{+}}-\frac{dp^{+}}{k^{+}+p^{+}}=\xi\,\frac{dp^{+}}{p^{+}}$$

so $\big[\frac{1}{p^{+}}\big]_{+}$ and $\big[\frac{1}{1-\xi}\big]_{+}$ are not the same distribution. Their $\delta$ coefficients are

$$\text{in }p^{+}:\ \log\frac{\vee-k^{+}}{\Lambda},\qquad\qquad \text{in }\xi:\ \log\frac{1-\xi_{0}}{1-\xi_{1}}\ \xrightarrow[\ \Lambda\ll k^{+}\ll\vee\ ]{}\ \log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}$$

differing by $\log\frac{\vee-k^{+}}{k^{+}}$, which is reshuffled into the $[\ ]_{+}$ remainder. The total is identical (both were checked against the same three-block integral). Pick whichever matches the rapidity variable in your evolution equation; for matching onto DGLAP the $\xi$ version is the natural one, and the rest of this note uses it.

5. Longitudinal momentum fraction

The $\mathfrak C_{1}$ vertex splits a parent of $S=k^{+}\!+p^{+}$ into the measured gluon ($k^{+}$) and an unmeasured one ($p^{+}$). The measured fraction is $\xi=k^{+}/S$, so

$$p^{+}=k^{+}\frac{1-\xi}{\xi},\qquad dp^{+}=-\frac{k^{+}}{\xi^{2}}\,d\xi,\qquad \frac{S}{k^{+}}=\frac{1}{\xi},\qquad \begin{cases}p^{+}=\Lambda &\to\ \xi_{1}=\frac{k^{+}}{k^{+}+\Lambda}\\[2pt] p^{+}=\vee-k^{+} &\to\ \xi_{0}=\frac{k^{+}}{\vee}\end{cases}$$

Three consequences, each a fragmentation signature:

$$\textbf{(i)}\quad \frac{p^{+}}{S^{2}}=\frac{\xi\bar\xi}{k^{+}},\qquad \frac{p^{+}}{k^{+2}}=\frac{1}{k^{+}}\frac{\bar\xi}{\xi},\qquad \frac{1}{p^{+}}=\frac{1}{k^{+}}\frac{\xi}{\bar\xi}$$ $$\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{p^{+}}{S^{2}}+\frac{p^{+}}{k^{+2}}+\frac{1}{p^{+}} =\frac{1}{k^{+}}\bigg[\xi\bar\xi+\frac{\bar\xi}{\xi}+\frac{\xi}{\bar\xi}\bigg]=\frac{P_{gg}(\xi)}{2N_{c}k^{+}}$$

— your bracket is the gluon splitting function, term by term.

$$\textbf{(ii)}\quad \int_{\Lambda}^{\vee-k^{+}}\!dp^{+}=k^{+}\!\!\int_{\xi_{0}}^{\xi_{1}}\!\frac{d\xi}{\xi^{2}} \qquad\text{— the fragmentation measure }\frac{d\xi}{\xi^{2}}$$ $$\textbf{(iii)}\quad e^{-ik\cdot r}e^{-ik\cdot r\,p^{+}/k^{+}}=e^{-i\frac{k_{\perp}}{\xi}\cdot r} \qquad\text{— the hard factor at }k_{\perp}/\xi$$

The Wilson lines sit at $x,x',y,y'$ with no $\xi$ dependence, which is the fourth signature. The Jacobian's $k^{+}$ cancels the $\frac{1}{k^{+}}$ of (i), so the prefactor $\mathcal P$ is unchanged. Written ascending in $\xi$ (so the signs are the same as in $p^{+}$):

$$\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}=\mathcal P\!\int_{\xi_{0}}^{\xi_{1}}\!\frac{d\xi}{2\pi}\frac{1}{\xi^{2}} \int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!e^{-i\kappa/\xi}\Big\{\mathcal C(\xi)\,\mathcal M\,\mathcal X-d_{\perp}\xi\bar\xi\,\mathcal R -\Big[\tfrac{\xi}{\bar\xi}+\tfrac{\bar\xi}{\xi}\Big]\mathcal X\Big\}\,\mathcal U, \qquad \mathcal C=\frac{P_{gg}}{2N_{c}}$$

6. The plus prescription in $\xi$

Only $\frac{\xi}{\bar\xi}$ is singular at $\xi\to1$, and it appears twice: inside $\mathcal C$ times $\mathcal M$, and in the last block times $-1$. They combine:

$$\frac{1}{\xi^{2}}e^{-i\kappa/\xi}\,\big[\mathcal M-1\big]\frac{\xi}{1-\xi}\,\mathcal X =\frac{h(\xi)}{1-\xi}\big[\mathcal M-1\big]\mathcal X,\qquad h(\xi)\equiv\frac{e^{-i\kappa/\xi}}{\xi},\quad h(1)=e^{-i\kappa}$$

$h(1)$ is finite, so

$$\int_{\xi_{0}}^{\xi_{1}}\!\!d\xi\,\frac{h(\xi)}{1-\xi}=h(1)\log\frac{1-\xi_{0}}{1-\xi_{1}} +\int_{\xi_{0}}^{\xi_{1}}\!\!d\xi\,\frac{h(\xi)-h(1)}{1-\xi} \;\longrightarrow\; e^{-i\kappa}\log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}+\int_{\xi_{0}}^{1}\!\!d\xi\,\frac{h(\xi)-h(1)}{1-\xi}$$

Define the regularised splitting function by exactly that subtraction:

$$\widehat{\mathcal C}(\xi)=\xi\bar\xi+\frac{\bar\xi}{\xi}+\frac{\xi}{[1-\xi]_{+}}=\frac{\widehat P_{gg}(\xi)}{2N_{c}}, \qquad \int_{\xi_{0}}^{1}\!\frac{d\xi}{\xi^{2}}\,e^{-i\kappa/\xi}\frac{\xi}{[1-\xi]_{+}} \equiv\int_{\xi_{0}}^{1}\!d\xi\,\frac{h(\xi)-h(1)}{1-\xi}$$

Everything else — $\xi\bar\xi$, $\frac{\bar\xi}{\xi}$, $\mathcal M$, $\mathcal X$, $\mathcal R$ and the Wilson lines — is regular at $\xi=1$ and is untouched.

7. Regrouping into three sectors

Split $\mathcal M=\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}+1-L_{r}$ and $\mathcal M-1=\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}-L_{r}$, then collect:

  • the $\delta(1-\xi)$ term (from §6) carries $\big[\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}-L_{r}\big]\log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}$;
  • the $\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}$ from $\mathcal M$ multiplies $\xi\bar\xi+\frac{\bar\xi}{\xi}$, and the $\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}$ from $\mathcal M-1$ multiplies the $[\ ]_{+}$ remainder — together exactly $\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}\,\widehat{\mathcal C}(\xi)$;
  • the remaining $1-L_{r}$ pieces plus the $-\frac{\bar\xi}{\xi}\mathcal X$ and $-d_{\perp}\xi\bar\xi\mathcal R$ collapse, using $\big[\xi\bar\xi+\frac{\bar\xi}{\xi}\big](1-L_{r})-\frac{\bar\xi}{\xi} =\xi\bar\xi-L_{r}\big[\xi\bar\xi+\frac{\bar\xi}{\xi}\big]$, into $-L_{r}\widehat{\mathcal C}(\xi)\mathcal X+\xi\bar\xi\big[\mathcal X-d_{\perp}\mathcal R\big]$.

Since $\frac{1}{\xi^{2}}\to1$ and $e^{-i\kappa/\xi}\to e^{-i\kappa}$ at $\xi=1$, all three fit under one integral.

$$\boxed{\;\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{4}}\frac{N_{c}}{k^{+}} \int_{\xi_{0}}^{1}\!\frac{d\xi}{\xi^{2}}\int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!e^{-i\frac{k_{\perp}}{\xi}\cdot(y'-y)}\; \Big\{\mathcal E+\mathcal D+\mathcal F\Big\}\;\mathcal U\;}$$ $$\mathcal E=\delta(1-\xi)\,\log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}\Big[\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}-L_{r}\Big]\mathcal X \qquad\textbf{evolution}$$ $$\mathcal D=\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}\;\widehat{\mathcal C}(\xi)\;\mathcal X \qquad\textbf{DGLAP}$$ $$\mathcal F=-\,L_{r}\,\widehat{\mathcal C}(\xi)\,\mathcal X\;+\;\xi(1-\xi)\Big[\mathcal X-d_{\perp}\mathcal R\Big] \qquad\textbf{finite}$$

8. What each sector is

Evolution

$$\frac{d^{3}N}{d^{3}k}\bigg|_{\rm evol}=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{5}}\frac{N_{c}}{k^{+}} \log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}\int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!e^{-ik_{\perp}\cdot(y'-y)}\; \frac{(x-y)\!\cdot\!(x'-y')}{(x-y)^{2}(x'-y')^{2}} \int\! d^{2}z\;\frac{(y-z)\!\cdot\!(y'-z)}{(y-z)^{2}(y'-z)^{2}}\;\mathcal U$$

The hard scale is $k_{\perp}$, not $k_{\perp}/\xi$ — at $\xi=1$ nothing is emitted into the measured direction. The $z$ integral is the real–emission BK/JIMWLK kernel, and $\log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}$ is the rapidity interval. It still carries $\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}$, which is that kernel's own large–$|z|$ divergence: $\int^{\infty}\!\frac{d^{2}z}{z^{2}}$. It cancels only when the Group II rows in which $U(z)$ survives the colour reduction are added, completing $\frac{(y-y')^{2}}{(y-z)^{2}(y'-z)^{2}}$, which is large–$z$ convergent. Do not absorb it into a fragmentation function — an FF cannot depend on $\Lambda$, and this term does.

DGLAP

$$\frac{d^{3}N}{d^{3}k}\bigg|_{\rm DGLAP}=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{4}}\frac{1}{k^{+}}\, \frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}\int_{\xi_{0}}^{1}\!\frac{d\xi}{\xi^{2}}\;\frac{\widehat P_{gg}(\xi)}{2} \int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!e^{-i\frac{k_{\perp}}{\xi}\cdot(y'-y)}\; \frac{(x-y)\!\cdot\!(x'-y')}{(x-y)^{2}(x'-y')^{2}}\;\mathcal U$$ $$=\frac{\alpha_{s}}{2\pi}\,\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}\int\!\frac{d\xi}{\xi^{2}}\;\frac{\widehat P_{gg}(\xi)}{2}\; \frac{d^{3}N^{\rm LO}}{d^{3}k}\!\left(\frac{k_{\perp}}{\xi}\right),\qquad \widehat P_{gg}(\xi)=2N_{c}\bigg[\xi\bar\xi+\frac{\bar\xi}{\xi}+\frac{\xi}{[1-\xi]_{+}}\bigg]$$

(the $\frac{\alpha_{s}}{2\pi}$ follows from $\frac{g^{4}/8\pi^{4}}{g^{2}/\pi^{2}}=\frac{g^{2}}{8\pi^{2}}$; the residual $\frac12$ on $\widehat P_{gg}$ depends on the LO normalisation convention and on whether the hermitian–conjugate row is counted separately — fix it against your own LO.) Remove the pole with

$$D_{g/g}(\xi,\mu_{F})=\delta(1-\xi)+\frac{\alpha_{s}}{2\pi}\bigg[-\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon} +\log\frac{\mu_{F}^{2}}{\mu^{2}}\bigg]\widehat P_{gg}(\xi)+O(\alpha_{s}^{2})$$

which sends $\mu\to\mu_{F}$ in the finite sector.

Finite

$$\frac{d^{3}N}{d^{3}k}\bigg|_{\rm fin}=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{4}}\frac{N_{c}}{k^{+}} \int_{\xi_{0}}^{1}\!\frac{d\xi}{\xi^{2}}\int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!e^{-i\frac{k_{\perp}}{\xi}\cdot(y'-y)} \Big\{-\log\!\big(4\pi^{2}\mu_{F}^{2}(y'-y)^{2}\big)\,\widehat{\mathcal C}(\xi)\,\mathcal X +\xi\bar\xi\big[\mathcal X-d_{\perp}\mathcal R\big]\Big\}\mathcal U$$

Two structures only. The first is $\widehat P_{gg}$ riding on the position–space logarithm: this is the NLO hard factor, and it does not factor out of the LO because $r=y'-y$ is integrated. The second is the pure transverse tensor

$$\xi\bar\xi\;X^{m}X'^{m'}\Big[\delta^{mm'}-d_{\perp}\hat r^{m}\hat r^{m'}\Big], \qquad X=\frac{x-y}{(x-y)^{2}},\quad X'=\frac{x'-y'}{(x'-y')^{2}},\quad \hat r=\frac{y'-y}{|y'-y|}$$

the azimuthal partner of the $\xi\bar\xi$ term of $P_{gg}$: no pole, no $\frac{1}{1-\xi}$, no logarithm, and it vanishes on averaging over $\hat r$ at $d_{\perp}=2$.

9. Loose ends

  • Where $\Lambda$ comes from. $\Theta\big(k^{+}_{\rm arg}-p^{+}_{\rm arg}-\Lambda\big)$ in your $\mathfrak C^{bca}_{1jki}$ becomes $\Theta(k^{+}-\Lambda)$ in this row — a condition on the measured gluon, always satisfied. The $\frac{1}{p^{+}}$ pole needs its own $\Theta(p^{+}-\Lambda)$; settle this before quoting the $\log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}$ coefficient against other rows.
  • The small–$\xi$ end. $\widehat P_{gg}\to\frac{2N_{c}}{\xi}$ against $\frac{d\xi}{\xi^{2}}$ is a $\xi^{-3}$ integrand at $\xi\to\xi_{0}=\frac{k^{+}}{\vee}$, held only by the oscillation of $e^{-ik_{\perp}\cdot r/\xi}$. Do the $y,y'$ integrals before sending $\vee\to\infty$.
  • Still to fix upstream: in (0.2) the overall $\frac{k^{+}+p^{+}}{k^{+}}$ is spurious (prefactor is $\frac{1}{k^{+}}$) and that factor belongs in the exponent, as (0.3) has it; in (0.18)/(0.19) $\mu^{2\epsilon}\to\mu^{-2\epsilon}$; delete $\int_{w,w'}$; $\int_{x,y,y'}\to\int_{x,x',y,y'}$; $U^{ce}(x)\to U^{ec}(x)$.
