Row V, second part: $2b$ completed, and a check of (1.11)–(1.16)

The UV extracted in $\overline{\rm MS}$, the $p^{+}$ integral done, and one correction to what I told you before

$\eta=\dfrac{p^{+}}{k^{+}}$,  $\bar\eta=1-\eta$,  $L=\log\dfrac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}$,  $\bar\mu^{2}=4\pi\mu^{2}e^{-\gamma_{E}}$,  $C_{\rm UV}(\eta)=\eta\bar\eta+\dfrac{\eta}{\bar\eta}+\dfrac{\bar\eta}{\eta}=\dfrac{\mathcal P_{gg}(\eta)}{2N_{c}}$,  $\mathcal W=\dfrac{(x'-w')\!\cdot\!(y-x)}{(x'-w')^{2}(y-x)^{2}}$

(1.11) through (1.16) are all correct — the six tensor blocks, the prefactor, the $\delta^{(2)}$, the $w$ integration, the six $p^{+}$ weights, the colour rewriting, the add-and-subtract and the split. Only presentational fixes remain (§1). Part $2b$ is completed in §3. Its UV pole carries the two moments $$\int_{\lambda}^{1-\lambda}\!C_{\rm UV}\,d\eta=2L-\frac{11}{6},\qquad \int_{\lambda}^{1-\lambda}\!C_{\rm UV}\log\bar\eta^{2}\,d\eta=-L^{2}+\frac{67}{18}-\frac{\pi^{2}}{3},$$ so you get a $\frac{2L}{\epsilon}$, the $\beta_{0}$-type constant $-\frac{11}{6\epsilon}$, and a $-\log^{2}\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}$.
A correction to what I said last time. I called the large-$|z|$ logarithm of this row a genuine infrared divergence needing cancellation against $\bar{\mathfrak B}_{2}$. That was computed with the phase dropped. With the phase kept, the $z$-dependence is $e^{i\bar\eta k\cdot z}$, so the large-$|z|$ region is cut at $|z|\sim\frac{1}{\bar\eta k_{\perp}}$ and there is no infrared divergence at all — it becomes $\log\bar\eta^{2}k_{\perp}^{2}$, and that is precisely what turns into the $-\log^{2}\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}$ above. The only pole in this row is the ultraviolet one at $z\to x$.

Four things to fix, all presentational but all real:

  1. The phases sit outside the integrals they depend on in (1.12)–(1.16). $e^{-ik\cdot(w'-z)}e^{-ik\cdot(z-x)p^{+}/k^{+}}$ is written before the $\Bigg\{$, i.e. outside both $\int_{\Lambda}^{k^{+}-\Lambda}dp^{+}$ and $\int_{x,y,z,x'}$, yet it depends on $z$, $x$ and $p^{+}$. Move it inside. (Cleanest: write it once as $\exp[-ik\cdot(w'-\bar\eta z-\eta x)]$ — see §2.)
  2. The colour factors and $\rho$'s sit outside $\int_{x,y,z,x'}$: the first $\Bigg\}$ closes right after the tensor bracket. It belongs at the very end.
  3. Braces unbalanced in (1.12)–(1.16): one $\Bigg\{$ opened, two $\Bigg\}$ closed. Fixing (2) fixes this.
  4. Write $2a$'s colour as $-f^{adc}f^{fbe}U^{db}(x)\big[U^{ce}(z)-U^{ce}(x)\big]$ (exact, same object). Then its UV finiteness is visible on the page instead of needing an argument.

2. Setting up the $z$ integral of $2b$

In $2b$ the colour factor $N_{c}\big[U^{af}(y)-U^{af}(x)\big]$ is $z$-independent, so the whole $z$-dependence is the kernel and the phase. Write the total phase in the form that makes this transparent:

$$e^{-ik\cdot(w'-z)}\,e^{-i\eta\,k\cdot(z-x)} =\exp\!\big[-ik\cdot(w'-\bar\eta z-\eta x)\big] =e^{-ik\cdot w'}\,e^{i\bar\eta\,k\cdot z}\,e^{i\eta\,k\cdot x}.$$

From §5 of the earlier note, the braces of (1.16) behave near $z\to x$ as

$$\xrightarrow{\;z\to x\;}\ \frac{C_{\rm UV}(\eta)}{k^{+}}\;\mathcal W\;\frac{1}{(z-x)^{2}}, \qquad \mathcal W=\frac{(x'-w')\!\cdot\!(y-x)}{(x'-w')^{2}(y-x)^{2}},$$

with all the $d_{\perp}$'s cancelling out of the residue. That last point matters here: because the residue carries no $d_{\perp}$, there is no $O(\epsilon)$ piece of it to multiply the $\frac{1}{\epsilon}$, so no extra finite term sneaks in from the $d_{\perp}$ bookkeeping.

The master integral

The $-\frac{11}{6}$ is $\beta_{0}$. $\frac{11N_{c}}{6}$ is exactly the $\delta(1-z)$ coefficient of $\mathcal P_{gg}$, i.e. $\frac{\beta_{0}}{2}$ at $n_{f}=0$.
