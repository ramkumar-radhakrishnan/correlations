Row IV: evolution, DGLAP and finite

The plus prescription in $\xi$, and the exact three-way split of the result

$\xi=\dfrac{k^{+}}{k^{+}\!+p^{+}}$,  $\bar\xi=1-\xi$,  $\xi_{0}=\dfrac{k^{+}}{\vee}$,  $r=y'-y$,  $\kappa=k_{\perp}\!\cdot\!r$,  $X=\dfrac{x-y}{(x-y)^{2}}$,  $X'=\dfrac{x'-y'}{(x'-y')^{2}}$,  $\hat r=\dfrac{r}{|r|}$,  $L_{r}=\log\big(4\pi^{2}\mu^{2}r^{2}\big)$

Yes, and the split is exact. Writing $\frac{1}{1-\xi}=\big[\frac{1}{1-\xi}\big]_{+}+\delta(1-\xi)\log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}$ separates your (0.24) into three pieces that sum back to it identically (checked to $10^{-14}$ at three kinematic points): $$\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}=\underbrace{\ \log\tfrac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}\otimes\text{BK kernel}\ }_{\text{evolution}} +\underbrace{\ \tfrac{1}{\bar\epsilon}\otimes\widehat P_{gg}(\xi)\ }_{\text{DGLAP}} +\underbrace{\ \text{NLO coefficient function}\ }_{\text{finite}}$$

1. The row, ascending in $\xi$

Flipping your limits (and the signs with them) so nothing has to be tracked:

$$\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}=\mathcal P\!\int_{\xi_{0}}^{\xi_{1}}\!\frac{d\xi}{2\pi}\,\frac{1}{\xi^{2}} \int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!e^{-i\kappa/\xi}\Big\{\mathcal C(\xi)\,\mathcal M\,\mathcal X -d_{\perp}\xi\bar\xi\,\mathcal R-\Big[\tfrac{\xi}{\bar\xi}+\tfrac{\bar\xi}{\xi}\Big]\mathcal X\Big\}\,\mathcal U$$ $$\mathcal P=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{4\pi^{3}}\frac{N_{c}}{k^{+}},\qquad \mathcal C(\xi)=\xi\bar\xi+\frac{\bar\xi}{\xi}+\frac{\xi}{\bar\xi}=\frac{P_{gg}(\xi)}{2N_{c}},\qquad \mathcal M=\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}+1-L_{r}$$ $$\mathcal X=(X\!\cdot\!X'),\quad \mathcal R=(X\!\cdot\!\hat r)(X'\!\cdot\!\hat r),\quad \xi_{1}=\frac{k^{+}}{k^{+}+\Lambda},\quad \mathcal U=\big[U^{e'c}(y')-U^{e'c}(x')\big]\big[U^{ec}(y)-U^{ec}(x)\big]\rho^{e'}(x')\rho^{e}(x)$$

2. Isolating the $\xi\to1$ pole

Only $\frac{\xi}{\bar\xi}$ is singular, and it appears twice — once inside $\mathcal C$ multiplied by $\mathcal M$, once in the last block multiplied by $-1$. They combine:

$$\frac{1}{\xi^{2}}e^{-i\kappa/\xi}\Big[\mathcal M-1\Big]\frac{\xi}{1-\xi}\,\mathcal X =\frac{h(\xi)}{1-\xi}\Big[\mathcal M-1\Big]\mathcal X,\qquad h(\xi)\equiv\frac{e^{-i\kappa/\xi}}{\xi}$$

and $h(1)=e^{-i\kappa}$ is finite, so the prescription is legitimate:

$$\int_{\xi_{0}}^{\xi_{1}}\!d\xi\,\frac{h(\xi)}{1-\xi} =h(1)\,\log\frac{1-\xi_{0}}{1-\xi_{1}}+\int_{\xi_{0}}^{\xi_{1}}\!d\xi\,\frac{h(\xi)-h(1)}{1-\xi} \;\xrightarrow[\ \Lambda\ll k^{+}\ll\vee\ ]{}\; e^{-i\kappa}\log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}+\int_{\xi_{0}}^{1}\!d\xi\,\frac{h(\xi)-h(1)}{1-\xi}$$

Everything else — $\xi\bar\xi$, $\frac{\bar\xi}{\xi}$, $\mathcal M$, $\mathcal X$, $\mathcal R$ and the Wilson lines — is regular at $\xi=1$.

Regularised splitting function. $$\widehat{\mathcal C}(\xi)\equiv\xi\bar\xi+\frac{\bar\xi}{\xi}+\frac{\xi}{[1-\xi]_{+}}=\frac{\widehat P_{gg}(\xi)}{2N_{c}}, \qquad \int_{\xi_{0}}^{1}\!d\xi\,\frac{e^{-i\kappa/\xi}}{\xi^{2}}\frac{\xi}{[1-\xi]_{+}}\equiv\int_{\xi_{0}}^{1}\!d\xi\,\frac{h(\xi)-h(1)}{1-\xi}$$

3. The three sectors

Substituting and regrouping by what absorbs each piece (write $\mathcal P_{0}\equiv\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{4}}\frac{N_{c}}{k^{+}}$, the $\frac{1}{2\pi}$ of the measure already included):

(A) Evolution sector

$$\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}\bigg|_{\rm evol}=\mathcal P_{0}\,\log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda} \int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!e^{-ik_{\perp}\cdot(y'-y)}\; \bigg[\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}-\log\big(4\pi^{2}\mu^{2}(y'-y)^{2}\big)\bigg]\; \frac{(x-y)\!\cdot\!(x'-y')}{(x-y)^{2}(x'-y')^{2}}\;\mathcal U$$

The bracket is exactly $\frac{1}{\pi}\,\delta_{mm'}\mathcal G^{mm'}$, so this is the $z$ integral put back:

$$=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{5}}\frac{N_{c}}{k^{+}}\log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda} \int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!e^{-ik_{\perp}\cdot(y'-y)}\frac{(x-y)\!\cdot\!(x'-y')}{(x-y)^{2}(x'-y')^{2}} \int\! d^{2}z\;\frac{(y-z)\!\cdot\!(y'-z)}{(y-z)^{2}(y'-z)^{2}}\;\mathcal U$$

Note the hard scale is $k_{\perp}$, not $k_{\perp}/\xi$: at $\xi=1$ nothing is emitted into the measured direction. This is the real-emission piece of the JIMWLK/BK kernel multiplied by the rapidity logarithm, i.e. $\partial_{\log(1/\Lambda)}$ of the dipole. Its $\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}$ is the large-$|z|$ end of that kernel and cancels once the companion rows supply the $\frac{1}{(y-z)^{2}}$ and $\frac{1}{(y'-z)^{2}}$ pieces that complete $\frac{(y-y')^{2}}{(y-z)^{2}(y'-z)^{2}}$.

(B) DGLAP sector

$$\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}\bigg|_{\rm DGLAP}=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{4}}\frac{1}{k^{+}}\; \frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}\int_{\xi_{0}}^{1}\!\frac{d\xi}{\xi^{2}}\;\frac{\widehat P_{gg}(\xi)}{2} \int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!e^{-i\frac{k_{\perp}}{\xi}\cdot(y'-y)}\; \frac{(x-y)\!\cdot\!(x'-y')}{(x-y)^{2}(x'-y')^{2}}\;\mathcal U$$ $$\widehat P_{gg}(\xi)=2N_{c}\bigg[\xi(1-\xi)+\frac{1-\xi}{\xi}+\frac{\xi}{[1-\xi]_{+}}\bigg]$$

Written with the LO structure $\mathcal N_{\rm LO}(q_{\perp})=\int_{x,x',y,y'}e^{-iq_{\perp}\cdot(y'-y)} \frac{(x-y)\cdot(x'-y')}{(x-y)^{2}(x'-y')^{2}}\mathcal U$ this is

$$=\frac{\alpha_{s}}{2\pi}\,\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}\int\!\frac{d\xi}{\xi^{2}}\;\frac{\widehat P_{gg}(\xi)}{2}\; \frac{d^{3}N^{\rm LO}}{d^{3}k}\!\left(\frac{k_{\perp}}{\xi}\right)$$

— textbook collinear factorisation for fragmentation. Remove it with

$$D_{g/g}(\xi,\mu_{F})=\delta(1-\xi)+\frac{\alpha_{s}}{2\pi}\bigg[-\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}+\log\frac{\mu_{F}^{2}}{\mu^{2}}\bigg]\widehat P_{gg}(\xi)+O(\alpha_{s}^{2})$$

which leaves $\mu\to\mu_{F}$ in the finite sector.

(C) Finite sector

$$\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}\bigg|_{\rm fin}=\mathcal P_{0}\int_{\xi_{0}}^{1}\!\frac{d\xi}{\xi^{2}} \int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!e^{-i\frac{k_{\perp}}{\xi}\cdot(y'-y)}\Bigg\{ -\log\big(4\pi^{2}\mu_{F}^{2}r^{2}\big)\,\widehat{\mathcal C}(\xi)\,(X\!\cdot\!X')$$ $$+\;\xi(1-\xi)\Big[(X\!\cdot\!X')-d_{\perp}(X\!\cdot\!\hat r)(X'\!\cdot\!\hat r)\Big]\Bigg\}\;\mathcal U$$

Two structures only. The first is the same $\widehat P_{gg}$ now riding on the position-space logarithm $\log(4\pi^{2}\mu_{F}^{2}r^{2})$ — the NLO hard factor, which does not factor out of $\mathcal N_{\rm LO}$ because $r$ is integrated. The second is the pure transverse tensor

$$X^{m}X'^{m'}\Big[\delta^{mm'}-d_{\perp}\hat r^{m}\hat r^{m'}\Big]\,\xi(1-\xi)$$

which has no pole, no $\frac{1}{1-\xi}$ and no $\log$: it is the azimuthal partner of the $\xi\bar\xi$ term of $P_{gg}$, and it vanishes on averaging over $\hat r$ at $d_{\perp}=2$.

5. Final answer

$$\boxed{\;\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}\bigg|_{\rm Row\,IV} =\frac{d^{3}N}{d^{3}k}\bigg|_{\rm evol}+\frac{d^{3}N}{d^{3}k}\bigg|_{\rm DGLAP}+\frac{d^{3}N}{d^{3}k}\bigg|_{\rm fin}\;}$$ $$\frac{d^{3}N}{d^{3}k}\bigg|_{\rm evol}=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{5}}\frac{N_{c}}{k^{+}} \log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}\int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!e^{-ik_{\perp}\cdot r}\, \frac{(x-y)\!\cdot\!(x'-y')}{(x-y)^{2}(x'-y')^{2}}\!\int\! d^{2}z\,\frac{(y-z)\!\cdot\!(y'-z)}{(y-z)^{2}(y'-z)^{2}}\,\mathcal U$$ $$\frac{d^{3}N}{d^{3}k}\bigg|_{\rm DGLAP}=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{4}}\frac{1}{k^{+}}\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon} \int_{\xi_{0}}^{1}\!\frac{d\xi}{\xi^{2}}\frac{\widehat P_{gg}(\xi)}{2}\int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!e^{-i\frac{k_{\perp}}{\xi}\cdot r} \frac{(x-y)\!\cdot\!(x'-y')}{(x-y)^{2}(x'-y')^{2}}\,\mathcal U$$ $$\frac{d^{3}N}{d^{3}k}\bigg|_{\rm fin}=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{4}}\frac{N_{c}}{k^{+}} \int_{\xi_{0}}^{1}\!\frac{d\xi}{\xi^{2}}\int_{x,x',y,y'}\!\!e^{-i\frac{k_{\perp}}{\xi}\cdot r} \Big\{-\log\big(4\pi^{2}\mu_{F}^{2}r^{2}\big)\widehat{\mathcal C}(\xi)\,\mathcal X +\xi\bar\xi\big[\mathcal X-d_{\perp}\mathcal R\big]\Big\}\,\mathcal U$$

6. Three things to keep in view

  • The evolution sector still carries $\frac{1}{\bar\epsilon}$. That is not a defect of this row: it is the unscreened large-$|z|$ end of the BK kernel, and it only cancels when the Group II rows in which $U(z)$ survives the colour reduction are added. Do not absorb it into $D_{g/g}$ — a fragmentation function cannot depend on $\Lambda$, and this term does.
  • The small-$\xi$ end. $\widehat P_{gg}\to\frac{2N_{c}}{\xi}$ against $\frac{d\xi}{\xi^{2}}$ gives a $\xi^{-3}$ integrand at $\xi\to\xi_{0}=\frac{k^{+}}{\vee}$, controlled only by the oscillation of $e^{-ik_{\perp}\cdot r/\xi}$. Do the $y,y'$ integrals before sending $\vee\to\infty$.
  • The $\Lambda$ in $\log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}$ is not the one supplied by the $\Theta$ in your $\mathfrak C_{1jki}$, which reduces to $\Theta(k^{+}-\Lambda)$ here. Settle where the $\xi\to1$ cutoff comes from before quoting this coefficient against the other rows.
