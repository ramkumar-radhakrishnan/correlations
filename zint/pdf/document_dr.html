Dimensional regularisation of the UV–projected row

$d_\perp=2-2\epsilon$, $\overline{\rm MS}$ — every step

$\displaystyle\int d^{2}r\;e^{iq\cdot r}\,\frac{1}{\bar\xi D}\,\frac{s\cdot P}{P^{2}r^{2}} \Big[\xi\bar\xi+\tfrac{\xi}{\bar\xi}+\tfrac{\bar\xi}{\xi}\Big]$, then $\int d\xi$

0. Conventions

$D=4-2\epsilon$ spacetime, so the transverse plane is $d_\perp=2-2\epsilon$; $g^{2}\to g^{2}\mu^{2\epsilon}$, with one power of $\mu^{2\epsilon}$ accompanying the divergent transverse integral (the other multiplies a finite quantity and is $O(\epsilon)$). The $\overline{\rm MS}$ scale is

$$\bar\mu^{2}\;\equiv\;4\pi\mu^{2}e^{-\gamma_E}.$$

Kinematics unchanged: $r=z-x$, $s=y-x$, $P=x'-w'$, $\xi=p^{+}/k^{+}$, $\bar\xi=1-\xi$, $q=\bar\xi k$, and

$$D=(r-s)^{2}+M^{2},\qquad M^{2}=\frac{\xi}{\bar\xi}s^{2},\qquad D_0\equiv D\big|_{r=0}=s^{2}+M^{2}=\frac{s^{2}}{\bar\xi},$$ $$C_{\rm UV}(\xi)=\xi\bar\xi+\frac{\xi}{\bar\xi}+\frac{\bar\xi}{\xi}=\frac{P_{gg}(\xi)}{2N_c}, \qquad \ell_k=\log\frac{k^{+}}{\Lambda}.$$

Step 0. $C_{\rm UV}$ carries no $O(\epsilon)$. The instantaneous $\delta^{im}$ vertex supplies an explicit $d_\perp$ and the angular average supplies $1/d_\perp$; they cancel, $c_1\langle S_1\rangle=\frac{d_\perp}{d_\perp}\xi\bar\xi\frac{s\cdot P}{r^{2}}$. So the projected bracket is exact in any $d_\perp$, and nothing multiplying the $1/\epsilon$ pole is itself $O(\epsilon)$ — no extra finite term is generated by $\epsilon\times\frac1\epsilon$.

1. The transverse integral is the only thing to regulate

$C_{\rm UV}(\xi)$ and $(s\cdot P)/P^{2}$ do not depend on $r$; the whole $r$–dependence is $1/(r^{2}D)$ and the phase. So the object to continue is

$$\mathcal G_\epsilon\;=\;\mu^{2\epsilon}\!\int\! d^{2-2\epsilon}r\;\frac{e^{iq\cdot r}}{r^{2}\,D}.$$

2. Split the divergence off exactly

$$\frac{1}{r^{2}D}=\frac{1}{r^{2}D_0}+\frac{1}{r^{2}}\Big(\frac1D-\frac1{D_0}\Big), \qquad \frac1D-\frac1{D_0}=\frac{D_0-D}{D\,D_0}=\frac{2\,r\!\cdot\!s-r^{2}}{D\,D_0},$$

and the second bracket vanishes linearly at $r=0$, so that piece is finite and may be taken at $\epsilon=0$:

$$\mathcal G_\epsilon=\frac{1}{D_0}\;\mu^{2\epsilon}\!\int\! d^{2-2\epsilon}r\;\frac{e^{iq\cdot r}}{r^{2}} \;+\;\frac{1}{D_0}\Big[\,2\,s\!\cdot\!\Psi-\Phi\,\Big].$$

3. The $d$–dimensional Fourier transform

$$\int d^{d}r\;\frac{e^{iq\cdot r}}{(r^{2})^{a}} =\frac{\pi^{d/2}\,2^{\,d-2a}\,\Gamma\!\big(\tfrac{d}{2}-a\big)}{\Gamma(a)}\,\big(q^{2}\big)^{a-d/2},$$

at $a=1$, $d=2-2\epsilon$, times $\mu^{2\epsilon}$:

$$\mu^{2\epsilon}\!\int\! d^{2-2\epsilon}r\;\frac{e^{iq\cdot r}}{r^{2}} =\pi^{1-\epsilon}2^{-2\epsilon}\Gamma(-\epsilon)\,\mu^{2\epsilon}(q^{2})^{\epsilon} =\pi\,\Gamma(-\epsilon)\left(\frac{q^{2}}{4\pi\mu^{2}}\right)^{\!\epsilon}.$$

(Checked against the known $d=3$ case, where the formula gives $2\pi^{2}/|q|$; §8.)

4. Expand in $\epsilon$

$\Gamma(-\epsilon)=-\frac1\epsilon-\gamma_E+O(\epsilon)$ and $\big(\tfrac{q^{2}}{4\pi\mu^{2}}\big)^{\epsilon}=1+\epsilon\log\tfrac{q^{2}}{4\pi\mu^{2}}+O(\epsilon^{2})$, so

$$\pi\,\Gamma(-\epsilon)\left(\frac{q^{2}}{4\pi\mu^{2}}\right)^{\!\epsilon} =-\pi\left[\frac{1}{\epsilon}+\gamma_E+\log\frac{q^{2}}{4\pi\mu^{2}}\right]+O(\epsilon) =-\pi\left[\frac{1}{\epsilon_{\rm UV}}+\log\frac{q^{2}}{\bar\mu^{2}}\right],$$

the $\gamma_E$ and $\log4\pi$ packaging into $\bar\mu^{2}=4\pi\mu^{2}e^{-\gamma_E}$ exactly.

Sign of the pole. It is $-1/\epsilon$, not $+1/\epsilon$. A short–distance ($r\to0$) singularity in coordinate space is regulated by $d_\perp>2$, i.e. by $\epsilon<0$ in the convention $d_\perp=2-2\epsilon$ tied to $D=4-2\epsilon$; the continuation returns $\Gamma(-\epsilon)$, whose pole is $-1/\epsilon$. For $\epsilon<0$ this is $+\pi/|\epsilon|>0$, matching the positive $\pi\log(1/m^{2})$ of a mass regulator. In the opposite convention $d_\perp=2+2\epsilon$ the same object reads $+1/\epsilon$.

5. $\overline{\rm MS}$ subtraction

$$\mathcal G_\epsilon=\frac{\pi}{D_0}\left[-\frac{1}{\epsilon_{\rm UV}}+\log\frac{\bar\mu^{2}}{q^{2}}+\tilde I(\xi)\right], \qquad \mathcal G_\epsilon\Big|_{\overline{\rm MS}}=\frac{\pi}{D_0}\left[\log\frac{\bar\mu^{2}}{q^{2}}+\tilde I(\xi)\right],$$ $$\tilde I(\xi)\equiv\frac{2\,s\!\cdot\!\Psi-\Phi}{\pi},\qquad \Phi=2\pi e^{iq\cdot s}K_0(M|q|),$$ $$s\!\cdot\!\Psi=\pi\!\int_0^1\!\!d\alpha\;e^{i\bar\alpha\,q\cdot s} \left[\bar\alpha\,s^{2}\,\frac{|q|}{\sqrt\Delta}K_1(\lambda)+i\,(q\!\cdot\!s)\,K_0(\lambda)\right], \quad \Delta=\bar\alpha\big(\alpha s^{2}+M^{2}\big),\ \lambda=|q|\sqrt\Delta .$$

Equivalently, as a single convergent parameter integral, $\;\tilde I=\hat I+\log\frac{q^{2}D_0\,e^{2\gamma_E}}{4}\;$ with $\;\hat I=D_0\int_0^1 dt\big[e^{itq\cdot s}\,|q|K_1(|q|\sqrt{\Delta_0})/\sqrt{\Delta_0}-\frac{1}{tD_0}\big]$, $\Delta_0=t[(1-t)s^{2}+M^{2}]$.

6. Back into the row — the $\bar\xi$ cancels

Since $\dfrac{1}{\bar\xi}\cdot\dfrac{1}{D_0}=\dfrac{\bar\xi}{\bar\xi s^{2}}=\dfrac{1}{s^{2}}$,

$$\frac{1}{\bar\xi}\,\frac{s\cdot P}{P^{2}}\,C_{\rm UV}(\xi)\,\mathcal G_\epsilon =\pi\,C_{\rm UV}(\xi)\;\frac{(x'-w')\cdot(y-x)}{(x'-w')^{2}(y-x)^{2}} \left[-\frac{1}{\epsilon_{\rm UV}}+\log\frac{\bar\mu^{2}}{\bar\xi^{2}k^{2}}+\tilde I(\xi)\right],$$

using $q=\bar\xi k$. The transverse structure is a product of two Weizsäcker–Williams kernels.

7. The $p^{+}$ integral

$\log\dfrac{\bar\mu^{2}}{\bar\xi^{2}k^{2}}=\log\dfrac{\bar\mu^{2}}{k^{2}}+\log\dfrac{1}{\bar\xi^{2}}$, and

$$\int_{\Lambda/k^{+}}^{1-\Lambda/k^{+}}\!\!d\xi\;C_{\rm UV}(\xi)=2\ell_k-\frac{11}{6}, \qquad \int_{\Lambda/k^{+}}^{1-\Lambda/k^{+}}\!\!d\xi\;C_{\rm UV}(\xi)\,\log\frac{1}{\bar\xi^{2}} =\ell_k^{2}+\frac{\pi^{2}}{3}-\frac{67}{18}.$$

The second is exactly minus Eq. (C.10) of arXiv:1610.03453, since $\log\bar\xi^{-2}=-\log(\xi\bar\xi)+\log(\xi/\bar\xi)$ and the second piece is odd under $\xi\leftrightarrow\bar\xi$ while $C_{\rm UV}$ is even. Both verified to 13 digits (§8).

8. Final result

Collecting the prefactor, $\dfrac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\dfrac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{4}k^{+}}\times\dfrac{\pi}{2\pi} =\dfrac{g^{4}}{128\pi^{7}k^{+}}=\dfrac{\alpha_s^{2}}{8\pi^{5}k^{+}}$, and pulling the $N_c$ out of the colour bracket:

$$\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm NLO}_{2}}{d^{3}k}=\frac{\alpha_s^{2}(\bar\mu)\,N_c}{8\pi^{5}k^{+}} \int_{x,y,x',w'}\!\!e^{-ik\cdot w'}e^{ik\cdot x}\; \frac{(x'-w')\cdot(y-x)}{(x'-w')^{2}(y-x)^{2}}\;\Big[\mathcal P+\mathcal F\Big]$$ $$\times\Big[U^{ab'}(x')-U^{ab'}(w')\Big]\Big[U^{af}(y)-U^{af}(x)\Big]\,\rho^{b'}\!(x')\,\rho^{f}\!(y),$$ $$\boxed{\;\mathcal P=-\frac{1}{\epsilon_{\rm UV}}\Big(2\ell_k-\frac{11}{6}\Big) \qquad\xrightarrow{\;\overline{\rm MS}\;}\qquad 0\;}$$ $$\boxed{\;\mathcal F=\Big(2\ell_k-\frac{11}{6}\Big)\log\frac{\bar\mu^{2}}{k^{2}} \;+\;\ell_k^{2}+\frac{\pi^{2}}{3}-\frac{67}{18} \;+\;\int_{\Lambda/k^{+}}^{1-\Lambda/k^{+}}\!\!d\xi\;C_{\rm UV}(\xi)\,\tilde I(\xi)\;}$$

Three readings.

  • The pole is the coupling renormalisation. $\mathcal P\times 2N_c=-\frac{1}{\epsilon}\big(4N_c\ell_k-\tfrac{11N_c}{3}\big) =-\frac{1}{\epsilon}\big(4N_c\ell_k-b\big)$ at $N_f=0$: the $\tfrac{11}{6}$ is the $b$–function coefficient, and this is what cancels against the $-2/\epsilon$ of the gluon self–energy counterterm. In $\overline{\rm MS}$ it is simply removed, together with $-\gamma_E+\log4\pi$, which is the content of $\bar\mu^{2}=4\pi\mu^{2}e^{-\gamma_E}$.
  • The scale in the logarithm is $k^{2}$, not the dipole size. After the $\bar\xi$ cancellation of §6 the only scale the projected integrand retains is $q=\bar\xi k$, so $\overline{\rm MS}$ delivers $\log(\bar\mu^{2}/k^{2})$ and the $\bar\xi$–dependence becomes the $\ell_k^{2}+\tfrac{\pi^{2}}{3}-\tfrac{67}{18}$. Trading it for $\log\big(\bar\mu^{2}(y-x)^{2}\big)$ is a rescheming, which moves $\log\big(k^{2}(y-x)^{2}\big)$ between the two and is not done here.
  • What is left over. $\tilde I(\xi)$ is finite and regulator–independent — the Bessel remainder of §5, unaffected by the continuation. Its $\xi$–integral is not elementary; its endpoints give $e^{ik\cdot(y-x)}\tfrac12\ell_k^{2}+c_0\ell_k+c_1$ as before.
