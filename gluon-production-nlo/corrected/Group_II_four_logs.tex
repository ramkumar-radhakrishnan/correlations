\section*{Group II, Row I: terms grouped by logarithm}
\noindent $N_{2a}+N_{2b}+N_{2c}+N_{2d}+N_{2e}+N_{2f}=(1)+(2)+(3)+(4)+(5)$.
\begin{itemize}
\item (1) and (2) collect the terms with $\log\frac{(x-z)^{2}}{(x^{\prime}-z)^{2}}$ and $\log\frac{(x-w)^{2}}{(x^{\prime}-w^{\prime})^{2}}$. Only $N_{2a}$ contributes to them.
\item (3) and (4) collect the terms with $\log\frac{(x-z)^{2}}{(x-w)^{2}}$ and $\log\frac{(x^{\prime}-z)^{2}}{(x^{\prime}-w^{\prime})^{2}}$, from $N_{2b},\dots,N_{2f}$.
\item (5) is the middle term of $N_{2f}$, which has no logarithm.
\end{itemize}
Every logarithmic term carries $1/\big[(x^{\prime}-w^{\prime})^{2}(x-z)^{2}-(x-w)^{2}(x^{\prime}-z)^{2}\big]$, which is pulled out. For $N_{2b}$ this uses $(x^{\prime}-w^{\prime})^{2}(x-z)^{2}(x-w)^{2}-(x-w)^{4}(x^{\prime}-z)^{2}=(x-w)^{2}\big[\cdots\big]$ and $(x^{\prime}-w^{\prime})^{4}(x-z)^{2}-(x-w)^{2}(x^{\prime}-z)^{2}(x^{\prime}-w^{\prime})^{2}=(x^{\prime}-w^{\prime})^{2}\big[\cdots\big]$, and $\frac{g^{4}d_{\perp}}{32\pi^{5}}=\frac{g^{4}}{8\pi^{5}}\frac{d_{\perp}}{4}$. The sum of (1)--(5) was checked against the original six terms at random points, with $d_{\perp}$ general.
