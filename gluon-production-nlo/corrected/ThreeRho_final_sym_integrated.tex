\section*{Three-$\rho$ result after symmetrisation: three-$\rho$ terms $+$ two-$\rho$ terms}
\noindent This is \texttt{ThreeRho\_symmetrised} with every $\delta^{(2)}$ integrated over a transverse point and the $O(\kappa^{2})$ one-$\rho$ terms dropped. The result is
\begin{equation}
\frac{d^{3}N^{3\rho}_{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}=\frac{d^{3}N^{3\rho}_{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}\Big|_{3\rho}+\frac{d^{3}N^{3\rho}_{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}\Big|_{2\rho}+{\rm c.c.}
\end{equation}
\begin{itemize}
\item $\big\{\rho^{a}(u)\rho^{b}(v)\rho^{c}(t)\big\}_{\rm sym}$ is the average over the six orderings of the three $\rho$'s; it is hermitian.
\item $[\rho^{a}(u),\rho^{b}(v)]=i\kappa f^{abc}\rho^{c}(u)\delta^{(2)}(u-v)$.
\item \textbf{How the $\delta^{(2)}$'s are integrated.} The point $x^{\prime}$ of the leading $\rho$ is always kept, and $\delta^{(2)}(u-v)$ is integrated over $v$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\delta^{(2)}(x^{\prime}-y)$: $\int_{y}$ sets $y=x^{\prime}$;
\item $\delta^{(2)}(x^{\prime}-x)$: $\int_{x}$ sets $x=x^{\prime}$;
\item $\delta^{(2)}(x-y)$: $\int_{y}$ sets $y=x$;
\item $\delta^{(2)}(x^{\prime}-y^{\prime})$: $\int_{y^{\prime}}$ sets $y^{\prime}=x^{\prime}$.
\end{itemize}
The substitution is made everywhere in that term: in the kernel, the phase, the colour factor and the $\rho$'s, and the integrated point is removed from the measure. Terms that come from the same three-$\rho$ term and carry the same $\delta^{(2)}$ are kept together under one kernel. Each group is labelled by the $\delta^{(2)}$ it came from.
\item The kernels are exactly those of \texttt{Three\_rho\_limiting.tex}. In the two terms with $f^{abd}$ in the prefactor, $f^{abd}$ is moved into the colour factor. Colour factors are left unsimplified after the substitution; for example, $f^{abc}f^{deg}U^{bd}(x)U^{ce}(x)=N_{c}U^{ag}(x)$ is not used.
\item An automated check confirmed that, in every two-$\rho$ block, the integrated point no longer appears anywhere.
\end{itemize}

\section{Remarks}
\begin{itemize}
\item The ``$+$c.c.'' (hermitian conjugate) applies to all terms, as in the original result.
\item \textbf{Dropped terms.} The $O(\kappa^{2})$ one-$\rho$ terms, with two $\delta^{(2)}$'s, are dropped, as requested. They are listed in \texttt{ThreeRho\_symmetrised}.
\item \textbf{Gaussian projectile.} For a Gaussian (MV) projectile the symmetrised three-$\rho$ terms average to zero, and only the two-$\rho$ terms remain.
\item The two-$\rho$ terms here should be added to the two-$\rho$ sum (\texttt{Two\_rho\_sum}).
\end{itemize}
