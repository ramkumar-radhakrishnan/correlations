\section{Final result}
Adding all the terms together:
\begin{align}
\frac{d^{3}N^{2\rho}_{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}=\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm GI,II}_{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}+\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm GI,V}_{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}+\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm GII,I}_{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}+\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm GII,IV}_{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}+\frac{d^{3}N^{\rm GIII}_{\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k},
\end{align}
where Group I and Group III include their c.c.\ and Group II does not. In these finite terms $d_{\perp}=2$.
