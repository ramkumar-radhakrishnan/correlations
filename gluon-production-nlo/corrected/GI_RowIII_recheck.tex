\begin{itemize}

\section{Step 2: there is no $i\pi$}
The framework is principal-value throughout: $\Omega$ is unitary and built from Rayleigh--Schr\"odinger denominators
$1/(E_g(k)-E_g(p))$ with no $i\epsilon$, and the Fourier transforms in Calculation\_main are ``defined as Cauchy principal value''
(e.g.\ $\int\frac{e^{ik\cdot X+ip\cdot Z}}{\xi k^2-p^2}=\frac{1}{\xi Z^2-X^2}$, real).
Group\_I's own closed forms reproduce the $p^{+}$ integral only with $|\cdot|$ (Step 1).

\medskip
\noindent\textcolor{red}{Therefore the rule $\log(-A)=\log A+i\pi$ used in Limiting\_Cases is not correct here}: $\log\frac{u}{1-u}+\log(1-u)\to\log|u/(1-u)|+\log|1-u|=\log u$ and
$\log(-u)\to\log u$. \textcolor{red}{The whole last block of Limiting\_Cases Row III,
$-\frac{g^4f^{abd}}{8\pi^4k^+}\int\frac{(x'-w')^i(y-z)^j}{(x'-w')^2(y-z)^2}\big[\frac{\delta^{ij}}{2(z-w)^2}+\dots\big]C(z)$, must be deleted.}
It was the only term with an unsuppressed $\delta^{ij}/2(z-w)^2$, i.e.\ the only UV-divergent term.
