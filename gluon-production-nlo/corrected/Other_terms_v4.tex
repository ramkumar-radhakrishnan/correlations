\section{Group I (with c.c.)}
{\color{red}\textbf{Reality.} Each term here is $T=\int e^{-ik\cdot\Delta}\,c\,\mathcal K\,\mathcal O$ with a real kernel $\mathcal K$, and the c.c.\ is added. For classical $\rho$: if $c$ is real, $T+T^{\dagger}=2c\int\cos(k\cdot\Delta)\,\mathcal K\,\mathcal O$; if $c=i\alpha$ with $\alpha$ real, $T+T^{\dagger}=2\alpha\int\sin(k\cdot\Delta)\,\mathcal K\,\mathcal O$, since $i\,e^{-ik\cdot\Delta}+{\rm c.c.}=2\sin(k\cdot\Delta)$. So every explicit $i$ disappears. Throughout, $\kappa\equiv k^{+}/\vee\in(0,1)$ and $u\equiv(x-z)^{2}/(x-w)^{2}$. The arguments $(1-\kappa)u+\kappa$ and $1-\kappa$ of the logarithms in Rows II, IV and V are positive. The only logarithms with possibly negative arguments are in Row III; they are treated there.}
