Without the correction, $r^{2}\times$(angle average) tends to a nonzero constant, $\propto\log(k^{+}/\Lambda)$, as $r=|z-w|\to0$.

\subsubsection*{Row III simplified: the negative-argument logarithms}
Let $u=\frac{(x-z)^{2}}{(x-w)^{2}}$. The logarithms come from the energy denominator of $\bar{\mathbb B}_{3}^{\dagger}$, $p^{+}(x-z)^{2}-k^{+}(x-w)^{2}-i0$, i.e.\ they are on the \emph{principal branch}, $\log(-A)=\log A+i\pi$ for $A>0$. No absolute values are needed. With $\theta$ the step function:
\begin{align}
&\log\frac{(x-z)^{2}}{(x-z)^{2}-(x-w)^{2}}=\log\frac{u}{|1-u|}+i\pi\,\theta(1-u),\qquad
\log\frac{(x-w)^{2}-(x-z)^{2}}{(x-w)^{2}}=\log|1-u|+i\pi\,\theta(u-1),\notag\\
&\log\frac{-(x-z)^{2}}{(x-w)^{2}-(x-z)^{2}}=\log\frac{u}{|1-u|}+i\pi\,\theta(1-u),\qquad
\log\frac{-(x-z)^{2}}{(x-w)^{2}}=\log u+i\pi .
\end{align}
The pairs that appear together add up to the same function for all $u>0$:
\begin{align}
&\log\frac{(x-z)^{2}}{(x-z)^{2}-(x-w)^{2}}+\log\frac{(x-w)^{2}-(x-z)^{2}}{(x-w)^{2}}=\log\frac{-(x-z)^{2}}{(x-w)^{2}-(x-z)^{2}}+\log\frac{(x-w)^{2}-(x-z)^{2}}{(x-w)^{2}}\notag\\&=\log\frac{-(x-z)^{2}}{(x-w)^{2}}=\log u+i\pi .
\end{align}
\begin{itemize}
\item In $N_{1}$, the first two terms have the same kernel and give the first pair.
\item The six terms of $N_{2}$ (log $\log\frac{-(x-z)^{2}}{(x-w)^{2}-(x-z)^{2}}$) and the first six terms of $N_{3a}$ (log $\log\frac{(x-w)^{2}-(x-z)^{2}}{(x-w)^{2}}$) contain the same six tensors with the same coefficients, so they give the second pair.
\end{itemize}
All logarithmic terms of $N_{1}+N_{2}+N_{3a}$ therefore multiply the single factor $\log u+i\pi$. With $X=x-w$, $Z=x-z$, $W=z-w=X-Z$, their tensor coefficient collapses to
\begin{align}
\frac{X^{2}+Z^{2}}{(X^{2}-Z^{2})^{2}}\delta^{ij}-\frac{2X^{i}Z^{j}}{X^{2}Z^{2}}+\frac{2}{W^{2}(X^{2}-Z^{2})}\Big[\frac{(W\cdot Z)X^{2}-(W\cdot X)Z^{2}}{X^{2}-Z^{2}}\delta^{ij}-W^{i}X^{j}+\frac{Z^{2}}{X^{2}}X^{i}W^{j}+\frac{X^{2}}{Z^{2}}W^{i}Z^{j}-Z^{i}W^{j}\Big]=2\,\mathcal K^{ij}_{\rm IV}.
\end{align}
For the $\delta^{ij}$ part, $\frac{(X^{2}+Z^{2})W^{2}+2(X\cdot Z)(X^{2}+Z^{2})-4X^{2}Z^{2}}{W^{2}(X^{2}-Z^{2})^{2}}=\frac{1}{W^{2}}$. For the rest, the difference of the two sides is $-W^{i}(X-Z)^{j}+(X-Z)^{i}W^{j}=0$. Here
\begin{equation}
\mathcal K^{ij}_{\rm IV}=\frac{\delta^{ij}}{2(z-w)^{2}}+\frac{(z-w)^{i}(x-z)^{j}}{(z-w)^{2}(x-z)^{2}}-\frac{(x-w)^{i}(z-w)^{j}}{(z-w)^{2}(x-w)^{2}}-\frac{(x-w)^{i}(x-z)^{j}}{(x-w)^{2}(x-z)^{2}}
