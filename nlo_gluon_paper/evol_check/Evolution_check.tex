\subsection{Group I, Row III (E1, E2)}
Write $\mathcal P=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^3}\int_{w,w'}e^{-ik(w'-w)}\,\frac{ig^4f^{abd}}{\pi^5}\,\frac{1}{k^+}\LK\int_{x,y,z,x'}$, and let $A=\AX$, $B_z=\BZ{z}$ be the two colour brackets of your file. The kernels are
\begin{align*}
Q_1&=\frac{(x'-w')\cdot(y-z)\,(z-w)\cdot(x-z)\,(x-w)^{2}}{(x'-w')^{2}(y-z)^{2}(z-w)^{2}\left[(x-w)^{2}-(x-z)^{2}\right]^{2}},&
Q_2&=\frac{(x'-w')\cdot(y-z)\,(z-w)\cdot(x-w)\,(x-z)^{2}}{(x'-w')^{2}(y-z)^{2}(z-w)^{2}\left[(x-w)^{2}-(x-z)^{2}\right]^{2}},\\
E&=\frac{(x'-w')\cdot(y-z)}{(x'-w')^{2}(y-z)^{2}(z-w)^{2}}\ \text{(edge kernel)},&
P&=\frac{(x'-w')\cdot(y-z)}{(x'-w')^{2}(y-z)^{2}}\,\frac{(x-z)^{2}+(x-w)^{2}}{\left[(x-z)^{2}-(x-w)^{2}\right]^{2}}.
\end{align*}
The $\log\frac{k^+}{\Lambda}$ terms of Row~III in your \texttt{Group\_I.tex} are, block by block:
\begin{align}
d^3N_1&:\ \mathcal P\Big[\tfrac{1}{16}\,\mathcal K(x'-w')\cdot\mathcal K(x-w)\,\mathcal K(x-z)\cdot\mathcal K(y-z)-\tfrac1{16}P+\tfrac1{16}P\Big]A\,B_z,\\
d^3N_2&:\ \mathcal P\,\Big(-\tfrac18\Big)\big[Q_1-Q_2\big]A\,B_z\qquad\text{(the bracket has the prefactor }-\tfrac{1}{(2\pi)^3}\text{ in your file)},\\
d^3N_{3a}&:\ \mathcal P\,\tfrac18\big[-E-Q_2+Q_1\big]A\,B_z.
\end{align}
The $P$ terms cancel ($+\log-\log$), and $Q_1,Q_2$ of $d^3N_2$ cancel those of $d^3N_{3a}$ term by term. What is left is
\begin{equation}
\text{Row III}\big|_{\log\frac{k^+}{\Lambda}}=\mathcal P\Big[\tfrac{1}{16}\,\mathcal K(x'-w')\cdot\mathcal K(x-w)\,\mathcal K(x-z)\cdot\mathcal K(y-z)\,A\,B_z-\tfrac18\,E\,A\,B_z\Big],
\end{equation}
and with fix F3 the edge term becomes $\mathcal D$:
\begin{equation}
-\tfrac18\,E\,A\,B_z\ \to\ -\tfrac18\,\mathcal K(x'-w')\cdot\mathcal K(y-w)\,\mathcal K(x-z)\cdot\mathcal K(w-z)\,A\,B_{\new{w}}.
\end{equation}
This is exactly what your list ``All three $\rho$ term with c.c.''\ uses (Table~\ref{tab:list}).
\begin{description}
\item[E1] \textbf{First form, $d^3N_2$.} You write $+\tfrac18[Q_1-Q_2]$, but it is $-\tfrac18[Q_1-Q_2]$. As written, the five terms add up to $\tfrac18[2Q_1-2Q_2-E]$ instead of $-\tfrac18E$. Corrected:
\begin{align}
\frac{d^{3}N_{2\,\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}&=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\int_{w,w'}e^{-ik(w'-w)}\Bigg\{\new{-}\frac{ig^{4}f^{abd}}{8\pi^{5}}\frac{1}{k^{+}}\LK\int_{x,y,z,x'}Q_1\,A\,B_z\notag\\&\quad\new{+}\,\frac{ig^{4}f^{abd}}{8\pi^{5}}\frac{1}{k^{+}}\LK\int_{x,y,z,x'}Q_2\,A\,B_z\notag\\
&\quad-\frac{ig^{4}f^{abd}}{8\pi^{5}}\frac{1}{k^{+}}\LK\int_{x,y,z,x'}\old{\frac{(x'-w')\cdot(y-z)}{(x'-w')^{2}(y-z)^{2}(z-w)^{2}}}\new{\frac{(x'-w')\cdot(y-w)\,(x-z)\cdot(w-z)}{(x'-w')^{2}(y-w)^{2}(x-z)^{2}(w-z)^{2}}}\,A\,B_{\old{z}\new{w}}\notag\\

\end{align}
The $Q_1$ and $Q_2$ terms now cancel in pairs, and the remaining (third) line is $\mathcal D$.
\item[E2] \textbf{Rewritten form, $d^3N_2$.} As written it is $-\tfrac1{16}\,\mathcal P\,P\,A\,B_z$, the $P$ term that cancels inside $d^3N_1$. After symmetrization it is exactly half of your first form as written. Corrected:
\begin{align}
\frac{d^{3}N_{2\,\rm NLO}}{d^{3}k}&=-\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{ig^{4}f^{abd}}{\old{16}\new{8}\pi^{5}}\frac{1}{k^{+}}\LK\int_{x,y,z,w,w',x'}e^{-ik(w'-w)}\notag\\&\qquad\old{\mathcal K(x'-w')\cdot\mathcal K(y-z)\frac{\left[(x-w)^{2}+(x-z)^{2}\right]}{\left[(x-w)^{2}-(x-z)^{2}\right]^{2}}}\notag\\
&\qquad\new{\mathcal K(x'-w')\cdot\mathcal K(y-w)\,\mathcal K(x-z)\cdot\mathcal K(w-z)}\notag\\&\qquad\AX\times\BZ{\old{z}\new{w}}.
\end{align}
\end{description}
With E1 and E2, both forms of Row~III equal the Group~I row with $\mathcal D$ ($6.9\times10^{-14}$ and $3.1\times10^{-14}$). Your $d^3N_1$ (both forms) is correct.
