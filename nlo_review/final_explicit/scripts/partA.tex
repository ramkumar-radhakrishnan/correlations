\section{Check of the author's finite terms against the verified versions}\label{sec:A}
\sloppy
\noindent Verified versions used: \texttt{fixed\_files/Final\_terms\_fixed.tex} (with \texttt{build/src}, \texttt{CHANGES\_fixed\_files.md}), \texttt{final\_checked/Final\_terms\_checked.tex}, \texttt{limits\_checked/Limiting\_cases\_final\_1\_checked.tex}, the paper draft \texttt{paper/draft/sec\_finite.tex}, \texttt{app\_finite.tex} and the fix notes \texttt{paper/notes/fix\_soft, fix\_poles\_dglap, fix\_singlet, fix\_tail, fix\_dabc.tex}. Every finite term of the author's file was compared, after $k\to-k$ (phase, Sec.~\ref{sec:conv}). Numerical checks: Sec.~\ref{sec:F}.

\paragraph{Phase.} The author's file has 57 $k$-dependent phases (BFKL 2, JIMWLK 2, DGLAP 1, RC 2, finite terms 50); all are $e^{-ik\cdot(\text{primed}-\text{unprimed})}$, including the recoil phases ($e^{-ik\cdot(w'-z)}e^{-ik\cdot(z-x)p^{+}/k^{+}}$, $e^{-ik\cdot(y'-w)}e^{-ik\cdot\frac{p^{+}}{k^{+}}(y'-z)}$, $e^{-ik\cdot(y'-y)}e^{-ik\cdot(y'-y)p^{+}/k^{+}}$): the author's convention was uniform. On the author's instruction all are changed to $e^{-ik\cdot(\text{unprimed}-\text{primed})}$ (red). The internal Fourier phases $e^{iq\cdot(w-y)}e^{ip\cdot(y-x)}$ of Group I Row II contain the loop momenta only and are unchanged.

\paragraph{Row by row.}

(ii) The arc-cosine argument $\frac{(x-w)^{2}-(x-w)\cdot(x-z)}{|x-w|\,|z-w|}=\frac{(x-w)\cdot(z-w)}{|x-w|\,|z-w|}$ is that of the verified $\mathcal{A}_{0}$; the bracket multiplying it is the verified one.
