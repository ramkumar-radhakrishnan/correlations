\subsection{Checks}\label{sec:F3}
\noindent All checks with \texttt{python3 -I}, random adjoint Wilson lines $U=2{\rm tr}(t^{a}Vt^{b}V^{\dagger})$ of $SU(3)$ and $SU(4)$ (Haar-random $V$, independent at every point), relative deviations (scripts in \texttt{scripts/}, logs \texttt{*.log}). Every check evaluates the colour factors \emph{as printed}: the \LaTeX\ source of this file is parsed (\texttt{s16\_lib.py}: index tensors $U^{ab}(x)$, $f^{abc}$, $d^{abc}$, $\delta^{ab}$, $N_{c}$; traces with $T^{e}$, $D^{h}$, $U$, $U^{\dagger}$ and differences) and compared with the colour tensors of our verified file.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Factorised $\rho$-explicit colour structures} (\texttt{s16c\_sec8\_colour.py}, \texttt{s16e\_final\_checks.py}): the 13 three-$\rho$ tensors $C^{abc}$ (Group I Rows II, III ($z$, $w$), V; Group II Row I (two); Group III Rows II, III, V, VI (each with $y'$ and $z$)) and the 8 two-$\rho$ colour factors (Group I Rows II, V and its $\frac{11}{6}$ line; Group II Row I second part, Row IV and its remnant; Group III Row III second part with $y'$ and $z$) equal the original ones of Sec.~6 to $\le1.6\times10^{-15}$ (162 colour blocks of Sec.~\ref{sec:F1} read from this file, plus the five equations of Group II Row I second part and the factors $L(v)\times R$ of Group III Row III second part, $\le1.8\times10^{-15}$). Control: one sign changed in the Group II Row I tensor gives $1.95$.
\item \textbf{Singlet forms} of Sec.~\ref{sec:F2}: every printed coefficient times trace bracket (61 items: 13 three-$\rho$, 33 contact, 15 genuine two-$\rho$) equals the average of the corresponding colour factor of Sec.~\ref{sec:F1} computed by direct contraction ($t\,C^{abc}d^{abc}$; $g\,C^{abc}f^{abc}$ with the Wilson lines at the coinciding charges; $\delta^{b'b}$ contraction): largest relative deviation $7\times10^{-14}$ (122 items $\times$ 3 configurations). The trace identities $f^{ijk}d^{lmn}O^{il}B^{jm}C^{kn}=i{\rm Tr}\{T^{h}O^{\dagger}CD^{h}B^{\dagger}O\}$ and $f^{ijk}f^{lmn}O^{il}B^{jm}C^{kn}={\rm Tr}\{T^{e}O^{\dagger}CT^{e}B^{\dagger}O\}$ for arbitrary $B$, $C$ and orthogonal $O$: $2.6\times10^{-15}$.
\item \textbf{Longitudinal integrals in $\xi$} (\texttt{s16d\_xi.py}, quadrature, random $k^{+}$, recoil arguments and complex transverse coefficients, plus prescriptions implemented by their definitions): Group I Row III $1.6\times10^{-16}$; Group I Row V three-$\rho$ $1.1\times10^{-16}$, two-$\rho$ $1.0\times10^{-15}$; Group II Row IV with $\xi=\frac{k^{+}}{p^{+}+k^{+}}$ (finite $\vee=4k^{+}$, $12k^{+}$; Jacobian $k^{+}/\xi^{2}$, limits $1\to k^{+}/\vee$) $1.3\times10^{-16}$, integrand pointwise $7\times10^{-15}$; Group III Rows III, V, VI and Row III second part (convergence factor $e^{-\epsilon\xi}=e^{-\epsilon p^{+}/k^{+}}$, $\epsilon=0.5,0.05$; the change of variables is exact for every $\epsilon$) $\le1.2\times10^{-12}$; at $\epsilon=0$ (Fourier quadrature) $\int_{0}^{\infty}d\xi\frac{e^{-is\xi}}{1+\xi}=e^{is}E_{1}(is)$ and $\int_{0}^{\infty}d\xi\big[\frac{1}{\xi(1+\xi)}\big]_{+}e^{-is\xi}=-\gamma_{E}-\log|s|-\frac{i\pi}{2}{\rm sgn}(s)-e^{is}E_{1}(is)$ to $5\times10^{-11}$. Control: the prefactor $\frac{1}{k^{+\,2}}$ kept after $dp^{+}\to d\xi$ (missing Jacobian) gives $0.47$. No $p^{+}$ is left in the equations of Secs.~\ref{sec:F1}, \ref{sec:F2}; the transformations of the kernels are string replacements with asserted counts (\texttt{make\_fixed2.py}), each of which is one of the identities checked here.
\item \textbf{Operator level} (\texttt{s16e}): the symmetrisation identities \eqref{eq:D-ord} (as corrected), \eqref{eq:D-r12}, \eqref{eq:D-r21} with $SU(3)$ charges $\rho^{a}=t^{a}$ on one to three sites (all coincidence patterns) and a random $C^{abc}$: $2.6\times10^{-16}$, $3.9\times10^{-16}$, $3.6\times10^{-16}$. The missing brackets of Eq.~\eqref{eq:D-II.I-3}: as typeset the colour tensor differs by $0.32$ (with the red braces $3\times10^{-16}$).
\item \textbf{The author's Sec.~7} (\texttt{s16b\_author2\_sec7.py}): all 61 colour items as printed equal the average of the Sec.~6 colour tensors (largest deviation $8\times10^{-15}$); Secs.~5--7 against the previous version and our verified file at the \TeX\ level: \texttt{s16a\_texcompare.py}.
\end{enumerate}
