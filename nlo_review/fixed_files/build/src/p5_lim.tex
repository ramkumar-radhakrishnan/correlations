\section{Phase convention (P5)}
\noindent[\textbf{New section; fix P5} (fix\_poles\_dglap Sec.~``Phase convention''; inventory of this file redone by script).] All 66 phases of the form $e^{-ik\dots}$ in this file (not counting this section and the orange removed equations) have the uniform form $e^{-ik\cdot(\text{unprimed}-\text{primed})}$, with the conjugated bracket at the primed point standing to the left: $e^{-ik\cdot(w-w')}$ (Groups I, II, III Rows I, II, block $\mathcal{S}$), $e^{-ik\cdot(z-w')}e^{-ik\cdot(x-z)\frac{p^{+}}{k^{+}}}$ and $e^{-ik\cdot(x-w')}e^{-ik\cdot(z-x)\frac{q^{+}}{k^{+}}}$ (Group I Row V), $e^{-ik\cdot(w-y')}e^{-ik\cdot(z-y')\frac{p^{+}}{k^{+}}}$ (Group III Rows III, V, VI), $e^{-ik\cdot(y-y')}$ (LO, RC, in the notes), $e^{-ik\cdot(y-y')/\xi}$ (DGLAP, Group II Row IV); no reversed phase is left (the reversed $e^{-ik\cdot(w'-w)}$ of the working files in \texttt{Main files/} gives each row at $-k$). One phase (Group I Row II) is written $e^{-ik(w-w')}$ without the dot; this is the same convention and is left unchanged. \emph{Effect of a mixed convention:} $k\to-k$ leaves the parts even in $k$ unchanged (all symmetrised two-$\rho$ terms, hence the Gaussian/colour-singlet averaged results of the next section) and flips the sign of the odd parts (the commutator remnants $\propto f^{abc}\rho^{c}$ and the symmetrised three-$\rho$ terms); with one convention everywhere the relative signs of the cubic terms of Groups I and III (e.g.\ in the pair cancellation of $\mathcal{S}$) are consistent.]}\par
