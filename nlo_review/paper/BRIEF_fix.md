Context. Be careful and precise; report only what you verified; never hide a problem; if something cannot be fixed, say so and say exactly why.

## The open problems (from the reviews)
P1 Group I Row III (6.4)-(6.7): at z -> w and z -> infinity the summed kernel is not integrable; traced to the log[(z-w)^2/(x-w)^2] terms of (6.7). Known so far: the last term of (6.7) is the Lambda -> 0 limit taken at fixed z != w of a term whose correct split is [log(k^+/Lambda) x JIMWLK term with U(w)] + recoil term -E(k.(z-w)) K(x,w,z) K(x'-w').K(y-w) C(w) (exact identity, verified); the third term of (6.7) = [LO kernel] - [recoil-shifted LO kernel] and has a -log(|z|/|x-w|) T/z^2 tail. The author's wish: "the one problematic term in Group I Row III should cancel with something" - show with what.
P2 Group III Rows III, V, VI (8.2)-(8.5): ln|z|/|z|^2 large-distance tails from the [k^+/(p^+(p^++k^+))]_+ remainder (a soft remainder of the plus prescription, real-emission kernel times a kinematic log); a matching virtual (real-minus-virtual) partner must be shown (cf. Altinoluk et al. 1411.2869 eqs. 3.7-3.13 where the remainder appears as [A(y-z)-A(ybar-z)]^2[s(y,z)s(z,ybar)-s(y,ybar)], falling like 1/|z|^4). Our earlier analysis: the 1/z^2 tails of all soft remainders (Group I Rows III, V; Group III Rows III, V, VI) cancel in the sum: z-independent parts on each recoil side separately, U(z)-linear parts only between the w and w' sides (uniform only if U(z) has a direction-independent limit at large distance). Also: the instantaneous -(1/k^+) delta delta terms of Group III converge as vee -> infinity only as distributions, int_0^infinity dp^+ e^{-iap^+} = pi delta(a) - i P(1/a): write them explicitly (review Q4).
P3 Virtual 11/3 terms and poles (review section 9, Q2): the reviewer says the virtual (alpha_s beta_0/4pi)[4/eps + L(x-y) + L(x'-y')] x LO from the wave function (I.89) is absent from the final result, and that the RC pole is collinear (IR) and cannot be removed by coupling renormalisation. Facts in our files: the 11/3 bracket of Group I Row II and the -11/6 bracket of Group I Row V (C_1^dag B_2, "2b UV") cancel exactly (pole, 2 gamma_E and log) after relabelling; the DGLAP pole and the RC (delta(1-xi)) pole are collinear. Required: a clear, correct account of all 1/eps poles (UV of the vertex correction, UV/IR character of the Row V term, coupling counterterm if any, collinear poles of the real splitting) and of what the "running coupling" term really is (cf. Kovner-Lublinsky-Skokov-Zhao 2308.15545 "Not all that is beta_0 is beta-function"), even though the final formula does not write poles.
P4 DGLAP form (review section 8): the ln xi^2 inside the logarithm is an artefact of rescaling u = (y'-y)/xi in 2 instead of d = 2 - eps dimensions; the MS-bar (Chirilli-Xiao-Yuan) form has [2 gamma_E + ln(r^2 mu^2/4)] without xi^2, and the xi -> 0 subtraction term must be written explicitly. Phase convention must be uniform (Row IV used e^{-ik(y'-y)}).
P5 Explicit ln|.| in Group I Row III; explicit distributional form in Group III; one phase convention everywhere.
P6 The final finite terms must be written, like the BFKL and JIMWLK terms, averaged over the projectile with <rho^a(x) rho^b(y)> = delta^{ab} n(x,y)/(N_c^2 - 1) (Gaussian/colour-singlet projectile; cubic terms are odd in k and vanish for a Gaussian projectile: state and verify) and in colour-singlet form with traces of adjoint Wilson lines.
