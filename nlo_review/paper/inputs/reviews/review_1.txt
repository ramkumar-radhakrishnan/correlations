2     How the checks were done
     Mechanical evaluation of your formulas. A small parser (texeval.py) reads Final terms.tex
      directly. It expands every bracket into monomials (K · K, K(a, b, c), 1/(a − b)2 , f abc , U ab (x), ρa (x),
      traces of T e , U , U † ) and contracts each monomial with einsum. Nothing was retyped, so transcription
      errors cannot enter the comparison.
     Lattice. A periodic 5 × 5 transverse lattice (minimal-image distances, 1/0 → 0 at coincident points),
      random SU(2) and SU(3) fundamental matrices converted to adjoint U ab = 2 tr(ta V tb V † ), and lattice
      momentum k = 2π(1, 2)/5. Any identity that holds algebraically in the continuum, pointwise or after
      relabelling dummy variables, then holds to round-off. It cannot hold by accident with random U .
     Projectile. Either a completely general complex tensor ⟨ρb (u)ρc (v)⟩ (no symmetry assumed, operator
      ordering kept), or the colour singlet ⟨ρb (u)ρc (v)⟩ = δ bc n(u, v)/(Nc2 − 1).
     Independent predictions. I wrote the BFKL and JIMWLK expectations from scratch (§4, §5), not
      from your Appendices E and F.

Your explanation. The β0 term arises because the real g → gg emission (Row IV) produces only Pgg+ .
You write Pgg+ = Pgg − β20 δ(1 − ξ), absorb the Pgg pole into DGLAP, and keep the compensating + β20 δ(1 − ξ)

cancel” is only the statement that the real emission has no δ(1 − ξ). Understood. Two physics points remain:
