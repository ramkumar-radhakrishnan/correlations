The finite terms are, row by row, the limit $\Lambda\to0$, $\vee\to\infty$ of
\begin{align}\label{eq:fin_def}
&\big[\text{row with the cut-offs}\big]\notag\\&\quad-\ln\frac{\vee}{k^+}\times\big[\text{its term in the BFKL list}\big]-\ln\frac{k^+}{\Lambda}\times\big[\text{its term in the JIMWLK list}\big]\,,
\end{align}
where the lists are those whose sums are Eqs.~\eqref{eq:BFKLrho} and~\eqref{eq:JIMWLKrho}. The terms $\ln(1-k^+/\vee)\times$(JIMWLK structure of the row) go with the JIMWLK term of the row, the collinear poles are treated as in Section~\ref{sec:dglap_rc}, and the DGLAP and running-coupling terms of Row~II.IV are those of Section~\ref{sec:dg_final}. Finite terms arise in Rows I.II, I.III, I.V, II.I, II.IV, III.II, III.III, III.V and III.VI. The rows with four charges only (I.I, II.II, II.III, III.IV) contribute to the evolution terms only, and the finite parts of Rows~I.IV and~III.I cancel identically (an operator identity, which therefore holds also in colour-singlet form).

We first give the finite terms for an arbitrary ordered correlator of the charges. The central point is the treatment of the soft remainders of the plus prescriptions in the rows in which the observed gluon recoils against a soft gluon, Sections~\ref{sec:fin_soft}--\ref{sec:fin_GIIII}; the complete list of the finite terms in this form is given in Appendix~\ref{app:finite}. We then average over the projectile and give the result in colour-singlet form, Section~\ref{sec:fin_singlet}.

\subsection{Conventions and notation}\label{sec:fin_conv}

\paragraph{Phase.} All terms are written with the phase $e^{-i\v k\cdot(\text{unprimed}-\text{primed})}$, with the conjugated factor at the primed points and to the left: $e^{-i\v k\cdot(\v w-\v w')}$; after the position of the observed gluon is integrated out, $e^{-i\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w')}e^{-i\v k\cdot(\v x-\v z)p^+/k^+}$ and $e^{-i\v k\cdot(\v x-\v w')}e^{-i\v k\cdot(\v z-\v x)q^+/k^+}$ (Row~I.V), $e^{-i\v k\cdot(\v w-\v y')}e^{-i\v k\cdot(\v z-\v y')p^+/k^+}$ (Group~III), $e^{-i\v k\cdot(\v y-\v y')}$ (LO, RC), $e^{-i\v k\cdot(\v y-\v y')/\xi}$ (DGLAP and the finite terms of Row~II.IV). The convention matters: a change of convention in a row amounts to $\v k\to-\v k$ there, which leaves unchanged the part of a term that is even in $\v k$ and flips the sign of its odd part. In the two-$\rho$ rows the odd part is the one-$\rho$ commutator remnant $\propto f^{abc}\rho^c$, which has zero average; in the three-$\rho$ rows it is the symmetrised three-$\rho$ part (up to one-$\rho$ double commutators), while the commutator (contact) parts of the ordered products are even (Section~\ref{sec:fin_singlet}). Mixing conventions between rows would give wrong relative signs of the three-$\rho$ terms, e.g.\ in the cancellation of their tails between Groups~I and~III below, and the wrong sign of the odd part of the spectrum for a projectile with a non-vanishing symmetrised three-point function.

\paragraph{Other conventions.} The complex conjugate is implied for Groups~I and~III, not for Group~II. The charges are operators in the order written. Energy denominators that vanish inside the integration region are principal values, and the corresponding logarithms are $\ln|\cdot|$ (Section~\ref{sec:fin_pv}). The common prefactor is
\begin{align}\label{eq:Cf}
\mathcal C_{\rm f}\equiv\frac{1}{(2\pi)^3}\,\frac{g^4}{16\pi^5}\,\frac{1}{k^+}=\frac{\mathcal C}{\pi}\,,
\end{align}
with $\mathcal C$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:dg_not}. We use $\Kv^i$ and $K(a,b,z)$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:kernels}, $P^i\equiv\Kv^i(x'-w')$, and the functions of one real variable ($t=p^+/k^+$; ${\rm Ci}$, ${\rm Si}$, $E_1$ the cosine, sine and exponential integrals)
\begin{align}\label{eq:recoilfns}
\mathcal E(s)&\equiv\int_0^1\frac{dt}{t}\big(e^{-ist}-1\big)={\rm Ci}(|s|)-\gamma_E-\ln|s|-i\,{\rm Si}(s)\,,\notag\\
\mathcal J(s)&\equiv\int_0^1dt\,e^{-ist}=\frac{1-e^{-is}}{is}\,,\notag\\
\Psi(s)&\equiv\int_0^\infty\frac{dt}{t(1+t)}\big(e^{-ist}-1\big)=\mathcal E_\infty(s)-\widehat{\mathcal E}(s)\,,\qquad
\widehat{\mathcal E}(s)\equiv\int_0^\infty\frac{dt}{1+t}\,e^{-ist}=e^{is}E_1(is)\,,\notag\\
\mathcal E_\infty(s)&\equiv-\gamma_E-\ln|s|-\frac{i\pi}{2}\,{\rm sgn}(s)\,.
\end{align}
For $|s|\to\infty$, $\mathcal E(s)=\mathcal E_\infty(s)-\frac{e^{-is}}{is}+O(s^{-2})$, $\Psi(s)=\mathcal E_\infty(s)-\frac{1}{is}+O(s^{-2})$ and $\widehat{\mathcal E}(s)\simeq\frac{1}{is}$; for $s\to0$, $\mathcal E(s)\simeq-is$, $\Psi(s)=O(s\ln|s|)$ and $\widehat{\mathcal E}(s)\simeq\mathcal E_\infty(s)$. All functions satisfy $f(-s)=f(s)^*$. $\mathcal E$ is the recoil function~\eqref{eq:Erecoil} of Group~I, where the soft integral runs up to $k^+$, and $\Psi$ its analogue in Group~III, where it runs to infinity; at large $|s|$ both tend to the same function $\mathcal E_\infty$, including its odd part.

\paragraph{Integrability.} A term is called integrable if its singularities at all coincidence points are integrable and if it decays faster than $1/z^2$ at large transverse position $z$ of a soft gluon (in practice like $\ln|z|/|z|^3$). A term $\propto e^{-i\v k\cdot\v z}/\v z^2$, in which the measured momentum flows through $\v z$, converges as a Fourier integral, as $\int_we^{-i\v k\cdot\v w}\Kv(x-w)$ does at LO, and is not counted as a tail.

\subsection{Soft remainders of the plus prescriptions}\label{sec:fin_soft}

\paragraph{Origin.} In Rows~I.III and~I.V the observed gluon in the amplitude, and in Rows~III.III, III.V and~III.VI in the conjugate amplitude, is part of a two-gluon state: a hard gluon at $\v w$ (resp.\ $\v w'$) and a gluon with $p^+=tk^+$ at $\v z$. The observed gluon is at the $+$-momentum weighted centre, so that the phase is $e^{-i\v k\cdot\v w}e^{-it\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)}$ (Group~I), resp.\ $e^{i\v k\cdot\v w'}e^{-it\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w')}$ (Group~III); in both cases the exponent is $-its$ with $s=\v k\cdot(\v z-\text{hard position})$. The longitudinal integral of a term $\frac{dp^+}{p^+}f(p^+)$ of a Group~I row is split as
\begin{align}\label{eq:softsplit}
\int_\Lambda^{k^+}\frac{dp^+}{p^+}\,e^{-i\frac{p^+}{k^+}s}f(p^+)&=\ln\frac{k^+}{\Lambda}\,f(0)+f(0)\,\mathcal E(s)\notag\\&\quad+\int_0^{k^+}\frac{dp^+}{p^+}\,e^{-i\frac{p^+}{k^+}s}\big[f(p^+)-f(0)\big]+O\Big(\frac{\Lambda}{k^+}\Big)\,,
\end{align}
and a term $\frac{k^+dp^+}{p^+(p^++k^+)}f(p^+)$ of a Group~III row in the same way, with $\Psi(s)$ in place of $\mathcal E(s)$ and with the additional $\ln(1-k^+/\vee)f(0)$, which goes with the JIMWLK structure. Here $f(0)$ is the coefficient of the row in the JIMWLK list, which is kept exactly as in Section~\ref{sec:JIMWLK}, and the last term is the plus-prescription term with the phase kept in the subtraction (the regular term), which is integrable by itself. The soft remainder of the row is $f(0)\,\Phi(s)$, with $\Phi=\mathcal E$ in Group~I and $\Phi=\Psi$ in Group~III. Since $|\Phi(s)|\simeq\ln|s|$ for $|s|\gg1$, the recoil cuts the soft integral off at $p^+\simeq k^+/|\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)|$, i.e.\ at $p^+/p_\perp\simeq k^+/|\v k|$ for $p_\perp\sim|\v z-\v w|^{-1}$: the soft gluon is restricted to rapidities below that of the observed gluon.

\paragraph{The double logarithms.} Each soft remainder behaves at large $z$ like $\frac{T}{\v z^2}\mathcal E_\infty(\v k\cdot\v z)$, where $T$ is the $1/\v z^2$ tail of $f(0)$, and is not integrable by itself. Before the split~\eqref{eq:softsplit}, i.e.\ at finite $\Lambda$, every such row contains $\pi\ln^2(k^+/\Lambda)\,T_{\rm row}+O(\ln(k^+/\Lambda))$, from the region in which the soft gluon is far from the observed one and is cut off only by the recoil; in the rows without recoil the tails multiply single logarithms only and are not cut at all. The coefficient of the double logarithm in the sum of all rows is $\sum_{\rm rows}T_{\rm row}$. This sum vanishes for an arbitrary projectile, in the symmetrised two-$\rho$ and three-$\rho$ sectors (numerically $|\sum T|<10^{-7}$ against single tails of order one to six, for $SU(2)$ and $SU(3)$; changing any one weight gives a sum of order one), and hence the double logarithms $\ln^2(k^+/\Lambda)$ cancel in the cross section. What is left at large distance in the logarithmic terms is only the infrared behaviour of the JIMWLK term for a non-singlet pair of charges, $\ln\frac{k^+}{\Lambda}\int\frac{d^2z}{\v z^2}\,C_A$, with $C_A$ minus the Casimir operator of the pair of charges acting on the LO structure; it vanishes for a colour-diagonal correlator, Eq.~\eqref{eq:singlet}, and is not affected by the finite terms. The vanishing of $\sum T$ is explained by the pair structure that we now describe.

\paragraph{The pairs.} The soft remainders cancel in pairs. Each Group~I term (soft gluon absorbed by the observed gluon in the amplitude: virtual, recoil on $\v w$) has one Group~III partner (soft gluon emitted by the observed gluon in the conjugate amplitude: real, recoil on $\v w'$) with the same coefficient, the same colour structure and the same kernel, except that $\v w$ is replaced by $\v w'$. With
\begin{align}\label{eq:CABCD}
\mathcal L^a&\equiv\big[U^{ab'}(x')-U^{ab'}(w')\big]\rho^{b'}(x')\,,\notag\\
C_A&\equiv f^{abd}\,\mathcal L^a\,U^{de}(x)\big[U^{bc}(w)-U^{bc}(y)\big]\rho^e(x)\rho^c(y)\,,\notag\\
C_D&\equiv f^{abd}\,\mathcal L^a\,U^{de}(x)\big[U^{bc}(z)-U^{bc}(y)\big]\rho^e(x)\rho^c(y)\,,\notag\\
C_B&\equiv\mathcal L^a\Big[f^{adc}\,U^{db}(z)\,U^{ce}(w)\,\{\rho^e(y),\rho^b(x)\}-f^{abc}\,U^{cd}(y)\,U^{be}(x)\,\{\rho^d(y),\rho^e(x)\}\Big]\,,\notag\\
C_C&\equiv\big[U^{ab'}(x')-U^{ab'}(w')\big]\Big[N_c\,U^{ah}(x)-f^{adc}f^{hbe}\,U^{db}(z)\,U^{ce}(w)\Big]\notag\\&\qquad\times\rho^{b'}(x')\,\rho^h(x)\,,
\end{align}
and with the Group~I variables (soft gluon at $\v z$, hard gluon at $\v w$, charges at $\v x'$, $\v x$, $\v y$; the Row~I.V terms relabelled accordingly, and in Group~III $\v y'$ renamed $\v w'$), each pair has the form
\begin{align}\label{eq:pair}
\text{pair }X:\qquad\mathcal C_{\rm f}\,\alpha_X\,V_X\,C_X\Big[\kappa_X(w)\,\mathcal E\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)\big)-\kappa_X(w')\,\Psi\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w')\big)\Big]\,,
\end{align}
under $\int_{w,w'}e^{-ik\cdot(w-w')}\int_{x,y,x',z}$, with the entries of Table~\ref{tab:pairs}.

That the Group~III member has the colour structure of its Group~I partner follows from four colour identities,
\begin{align}\label{eq:colid}
&f^{c'ca}\,\mathcal L_{\rm V}^{c'}\,\mathcal R_{\rm V}^{ac}=-C_A\,,\qquad
f^{c'd'a}\,\mathcal L^{c'd'b}(w')\,\mathcal R_1^{ab}=-C_B\,,\notag\\
&f^{c'd'a}\,\mathcal L^{c'd'b}(w')\,\mathcal R_2^{ab}=-C_C\,,\qquad
f^{c'd'a}\,\mathcal L^{c'd'b}(w')\,\mathcal R_{\rm VI}^{ab}\big|_{x\leftrightarrow y}=+C_D\,,
\end{align}
where the left-hand sides are the colour structures of Rows~III.V, III.III and~III.VI (Appendix~\ref{app:finite}) with $\v y'\to\v w'$. They follow from $U^{d'b}(z)U^{cb}(z)=\delta^{d'c}$, $f^{c'd'a}f^{dd'a}=N_c\delta^{c'd}$ and a relabelling of colour indices only; no charges are reordered, so they are operator identities, valid for any $U$ at any $\v z$. They were also verified numerically, together with the statement that each entry of Table~\ref{tab:pairs} equals the row's own coefficient $f(0)$, in the two- and three-$\rho$ sectors for $SU(2)$ and $SU(3)$ (to $2\times10^{-15}$).

\paragraph{The two recoil sides combine without a condition on the target.} Each pair can be written as
\begin{align}\label{eq:rmv}
&\kappa(w)\,\mathcal E\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)\big)-\kappa(w')\,\Psi\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w')\big)
&=\underbrace{\big[\kappa(w)-\kappa(w')\big]\mathcal E\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)\big)}_{\text{real minus virtual}\,\times\,\text{kinematic logarithm}}\notag\\&\quad
+\underbrace{\kappa(w')\Big[\mathcal E\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)\big)-\Psi\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w')\big)\Big]}_{\text{mismatch of the recoil on }\v w\text{ and on }\v w'}\,.
\end{align}
In the first term $\kappa(w)-\kappa(w')=O(|\v w-\v w'|/|\v z|^3)$ while $|\mathcal E|\le\ln|z|+{\rm const}$; it has the real-minus-virtual structure of the remainder $[A(y-z)-A(\bar y-z)]^2[s(y,z)s(z,\bar y)-s(y,\bar y)]$ found in forward production~\cite{Altinoluk:2014eka} and is integrable. In the second term $\mathcal E(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w))-\Psi(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w'))=\ln\frac{|\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w')|}{|\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)|}+O(1/|\v k\cdot\v z|)$: the odd parts $-\frac{i\pi}{2}{\rm sgn}$ are the same on the two sides and cancel, and the remainder is $O(1/|\v z|)$ outside the strip $|\v k\cdot\v z|\lesssim|\v k||\v w-\v w'|$ and bounded by logarithms inside it, which gives a convergent integral. The colour structure $C_X$, with its whole dependence on $U(\v z)$, is a common factor of both terms. Therefore the tails of the two members of a pair are $\pm T_X/\v z^2$ exactly, as operators and for every value of $U(\v z)$: the parts linear in $U(\v z)$, the $\v z$-independent parts and the parts with $U(\v z)U^T(\v z)$ cancel together, and nothing has to be assumed about the Wilson line at large distance beyond $|U^{ab}|\le1$; for a target of finite size, $U(\v z)\to1$, the cancellation is a special case. (In an earlier pairing of the Group~I terms with the complex-conjugate Group~III terms, which also have their recoil on $\v w$ but carry $\Psi^*$, a remainder $\propto i\pi\,{\rm sgn}(\v k\cdot\v z)\,T^{(1)}/\v z^2$, linear in $U(\v z)$, seemed to survive, which would have required a direction-independent limit of $U(\v z)$; with the pairing~\eqref{eq:pair} it is absent.) The complex-conjugate half is the complex conjugate of each pair, with $\mathcal E^*$ and $\Psi^*$, and cancels pair by pair in the same way. Numerically, $\v z^2$ times the sum of the two members decreases like $1/|\v z|$ in every direction, i.e.\ the sum is $O(|\v z|^{-3})$, for all four pairs, $SU(2)$ and $SU(3)$, in the two- and three-$\rho$ sectors, in the colour-singlet projection, with $U(\v z)=1$ and with a random $U(\v z)$ in every direction; the angular mean of $[\kappa_X(w)-\kappa_X(w')]V_XC_X$ falls like $|\v z|^{-4}$.

\paragraph{The original form of the last term of $N_{3b}$.} In Row~I.III the term that becomes the Group~I member of pair~A was originally obtained as the limit $\Lambda\to0$ at fixed $\v z\neq\v w$, $-\frac{1}{2}\ln\frac{(z-w)^2}{(x-w)^2}\frac{P\cdot\Kv(y-z)}{(z-w)^2}$ times $C_D$ in units of $2i\,\mathcal C_{\rm f}$; its $\v z$ integral diverges at $\v z\to\v w$. Together with the term $-\big[L+\ln(1-\frac{\Lambda}{k^+})\big]\frac{P\cdot\Kv(y-z)}{(z-w)^2}C_D$ of $N_{3a}$ it is, with $\Phi(z)\equiv P\cdot\Kv(y-z)\,C_D(z)$, $\lambda\equiv\Lambda/k^+$ and exactly in $\lambda$,
\begin{align}\label{eq:N3bexact}
\int_we^{-ik\cdot w}\Big(-\frac12\Big)\int_z\frac{\mathcal G_\Lambda}{(z-w)^2}\,\Phi(z)
&=-\int_{w,z}e^{-ik\cdot w}\,K(x,w,z)\,\Phi(w)\int_\lambda^{1-\lambda}\frac{dt}{t}\,e^{-it\,k\cdot(z-w)}\notag\\
&=-\int_{w,z}e^{-ik\cdot w}\,K(x,w,z)\,\Phi(w)\notag\\&\qquad\times\Big[L+\mathcal E\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)\big)+O(\lambda)\Big]\,,
\end{align}
where $\mathcal G_\Lambda=\ln\big\{[(x-w)+\frac{1-\lambda}{\lambda}(z-w)]^2/[(x-w)+\frac{\lambda}{1-\lambda}(z-w)]^2\big\}$, and the first equality follows from the substitution of the position of the soft gluon for that of the hard gluon before the absorption, followed by a change of the variable $\v w$ to the hard position under the Fourier integral. The term with $L$ is the JIMWLK-list term of the row in its integrable form (kernel $-P\cdot\Kv(y-w)K(x,w,z)$ with $U(\v w)$, i.e.\ $C_A$), and the rest is the recoil term, the Group~I member of pair~A. The pointwise form with $U(\v z)$ is the same term written in the recoil variable, in which the split into $L\times$(kernel) and a left-over does not exist.

\subsection{The problematic term of Row I.III and what cancels it}\label{sec:fin_GIIII}

The third term of $N_{3b}$ of Row~I.III is of a different kind. With $a_t=(x-w)-t(x-z)$,
\begin{align}\label{eq:ell}
\ell(z,w,x)\equiv-\frac12\ln\frac{(z-w)^2}{(x-w)^2}=-\int_0^1\frac{dt}{t}\Big[1-\frac{(x-w)\cdot a_t}{a_t^2}\Big]\,,
\end{align}
so that $\ell\,\Kv^i(x-w)$ is the LO kernel minus the LO kernel with the source shifted by the recoil; the soft integral is cut off at $t\simeq|\v x-\v w|/|\v z-\v w|$, without a phase, and nothing of this term is in the JIMWLK list. The term is $2i\,\mathcal C_{\rm f}\,\ell\,K(y,w,z)\,P\cdot\Kv(x-w)\,C_D$; it is integrable at $\v z\to\v w$ ($\ell K(y,w,z)\sim\ln|\v z-\v w|/|\v z-\v w|$), but at large $z$ it tends to $\frac{T_D}{\v z^2}\ell$ with $\ell\to-\ln\frac{|z|}{|x-w|}$, an isotropic tail without the odd part of $\mathcal E_\infty$. Its $\v z$-independent part is $-2iP\cdot\Kv(x-w)f^{abd}\mathcal L^aU^{de}(x)U^{bc}(y)\rho^e(x)\rho^c(y)$, its part linear in $U(\v z)$ is $+2iP\cdot\Kv(x-w)f^{abd}\mathcal L^aU^{de}(x)U^{bc}(z)\rho^e(x)\rho^c(y)$. We split
\begin{align}
\ell=\mathcal E\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)\big)+\big[\ell-\mathcal E\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)\big)\big]\,.
\end{align}
The first part is the Group~I member of pair~D. The second part is combined with the other 24 terms of Row~I.III, whose sum is the combined kernel $\mathcal M^{ij}$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:Mij} below, into
\begin{align}\label{eq:Mhat}
\widehat{\mathcal M}^i(x,y,z,w)\equiv\Kv^j(y-z)\,\mathcal M^{ij}+\Big[\ell(z,w,x)-\mathcal E\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)\big)\Big]K(y,w,z)\,\Kv^i(x-w)\,.
\end{align}
At fixed $\v x,\v y,\v x',\v w'$ and $|z|\to\infty$,
\begin{align}\label{eq:Mhatclaim}
\int d^2w\,e^{-i\v k\cdot\v w}\,\widehat{\mathcal M}^i(x,y,z,w)=O\Big(\frac{\ln|z|}{|z|^3}\Big)+\big(\text{terms}\propto e^{-i\v k\cdot\v z}\big)\,,
\end{align}
i.e.\ after the $\v w$ integration there is no non-oscillating $1/\v z^2$ term in any direction, for any $U(\v z)$: the colour factor $\mathcal L^aC_D$ does not depend on $\v w$ and is a spectator. The argument is as follows.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The 24-term kernel has the large-$z$ form
\begin{align}\label{eq:saw}
\Kv^j(y-z)\,\mathcal M^{ij}\;\xrightarrow[|z|\to\infty]{}\;\frac{\varphi}{\v z^2}\,\frac{e_\perp^i}{|x-w|}+O\Big(\frac{\ln|z|}{|z|^3}\Big)\,,\qquad-\pi<\varphi<\pi\,,
\end{align}
where $\varphi$ is the angle from $\v x-\v w$ to $\v z$ and $e_\perp$ the unit vector $(\v x-\v w)/|\v x-\v w|$ rotated by $+\frac\pi2$. It comes from the terms with $\mathcal A_0$, has zero angular mean, but does not vanish pointwise; Eq.~\eqref{eq:saw} was established numerically.
\item By Eq.~\eqref{eq:recoilfns} and $K(y,w,z)\to1/\v z^2$, the second term of Eq.~\eqref{eq:Mhat} tends to $\frac{\Kv^i(x-w)}{\v z^2}\big[\gamma_E+\ln(|\v k||\v x-\v w|)+c(\theta)\big]$, up to terms $\propto e^{-i\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)}$, with $c(\theta)=\ln|\cos\theta|+\frac{i\pi}{2}{\rm sgn}(\cos\theta)$ and $\theta$ the angle between $\v k$ and $\v z$.
\item With $\v r=\v x-\v w$ and $e^{-i\v k\cdot\v w}=e^{-i\v k\cdot\v x}e^{i\v k\cdot\v r}$, the radial integrals $\int_0^\infty dr\,e^{i\kappa r}=i\mathcal P\frac1\kappa+\pi\delta(\kappa)$ and $\int_0^\infty dr\,e^{i\kappa r}[\gamma_E+\ln(|\v k|r)]=\frac{i}{\kappa}\big[-\ln\frac{|\kappa|}{|\v k|}+\frac{i\pi}{2}{\rm sgn}\,\kappa\big]$, $\kappa=|\v k|\cos\alpha$ ($\alpha$ the angle of $\v r$ with $\v k$; with a convergence factor $e^{-\epsilon r}$), followed by the angular integrals, give
\begin{align}\label{eq:sawid}
\int d^2r\,e^{i\v k\cdot\v r}\,\Kv^i(r)\Big[\gamma_E+\ln(|\v k||\v r|)+c(\theta)\Big]&=+\frac{2\pi i\,\hat k^i}{|\v k|}\,\chi(\theta)\,,\notag\\
\int d^2r\,e^{i\v k\cdot\v r}\,\varphi\,\frac{e_\perp^i}{|\v r|}&=-\frac{2\pi i\,\hat k^i}{|\v k|}\,\chi(\theta)\,,\notag\\
\chi(\theta)&\equiv\ln|2\cos\theta|+\frac{i\pi}{2}\,{\rm sgn}(\cos\theta)\,.
\end{align}
For the first integral, $\int_0^{2\pi}d\alpha\,[-\ln|\cos\alpha|]=2\pi\ln2$, and the component along $\sin\alpha$ is odd. For the second, $\varphi=\theta-\alpha$ (mod $2\pi$) has a jump of $2\pi$ at $\alpha=\theta+\pi$; $\mathcal P\!\int d\alpha\,\varphi\tan\alpha=2\pi\ln|2\cos\theta|$ by parts, and the terms $\pi\delta(\kappa)$ at $\alpha=\pm\frac\pi2$ give $\frac{\pi^2}{|\v k|}{\rm sgn}(\cos\theta)$. The two integrals cancel. Since $\chi$ has zero angular mean, each of them alone is only conditionally convergent (angular integral first), and not convergent at all if $U(\v z)$ depends on the direction of $\v z$.
\end{enumerate}
Eq.~\eqref{eq:sawid} was checked numerically with the convergence factor $e^{-\epsilon r}$ (the sum of the two integrals vanishes like $\epsilon$, each being up to $8$ in modulus), and Eq.~\eqref{eq:Mhatclaim} with the full kernels: at $|\v z|=2560$ the two parts of $\v z^2\int_we^{-ik\cdot w}\widehat{\mathcal M}$ are $6.933-2.690i$ and $-6.906+2.690i$, against the prediction $\pm(6.909-2.687i)$, and $\v z^2$ times their sum decreases like $1/|\v z|$ in all directions tested.

\paragraph{Answer.} The third term of $N_{3b}$, $2i\,\mathcal C_{\rm f}\,\ell\,K(y,w,z)\,P\cdot\Kv(x-w)\,C_D$, with tail $-\frac{T_D}{\v z^2}\ln\frac{|z|}{|x-w|}$, is cancelled (1) in its $\ln|z|$ part, for the $U(\v z)$-independent and the $U(\v z)$-linear colour parts alike, by the soft remainder of Row~III.VI, $-2i\,\mathcal C_{\rm f}\,\Psi\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w')\big)K(y,w',z)\,P\cdot\Kv(x-w)\,C_D$, which has the same coefficient and colour structure and the kernel with $\v w\to\v w'$ (pair~D); and (2) in its remaining $1/\v z^2$ part, $\frac{T_D}{\v z^2}[\gamma_E+\ln(|\v k||\v x-\v w|)+c(\theta)]$, by the tail~\eqref{eq:saw} of the other 24 terms of the same row, after the integration over $\v w$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:sawid}. The last term of $N_{3b}$, in its recoil form~\eqref{eq:N3bexact}, is cancelled by the soft remainder of Row~III.V (pair~A), with the coefficient $-2i$ on both sides and the kernels $K(x,w,z)$ and $K(x,w',z)$. At $\v z\to\v w$ both terms are integrable.

\subsection{Principal values}\label{sec:fin_pv}

In $\mf B_3$ the denominators $p^+\v k^2-k^+\v p^2$ (momentum space) and $p^+(\v x-\v u)^2-k^+(\v x-\v z)^2$ (coordinate space) are differences of the light-cone energies $\v k^2/2k^+$ and $\v p^2/2p^+$ of two one-gluon states connected by the static charge $\rho(\v k-\v p)$, which transfers transverse momentum but no light-cone energy; they vanish on the energy shell. The dressing operator is constructed in stationary perturbation theory, and these denominators are taken as principal values; this is the prescription of the calculation (Appendix~\ref{app:distributions}). With $A=(\v x-\v w)^2$, $B=(\v x-\v z)^2$, $\int_{p_1}^{p_2}dp^+\,\mathcal P\frac{1}{p^+B-k^+A}=\frac1B\ln\big|\frac{p_2B-k^+A}{p_1B-k^+A}\big|$, and in the separate terms $N_1$, $N_2$, $N_{3a}$ of Row~I.III every logarithm whose argument changes sign is a logarithm of the absolute value,
\begin{align}
\ln\Big|\frac{(x-z)^2}{(x-z)^2-(x-w)^2}\Big|\quad(N_1,\,N_2)\,,\qquad\ln\Big|\frac{(x-w)^2-(x-z)^2}{(x-w)^2}\Big|\quad(N_1,\,N_{3a})\,;
\end{align}
the other logarithms, $\ln\frac{(x-z)^2}{(x-w)^2}$ and $\ln\frac{(z-w)^2}{(x-w)^2}$, have positive arguments, and $\mathcal A_0$ is real. In the combined kernel~\eqref{eq:Mij} these logarithms have combined into $\ln\frac{(x-z)^2}{(x-w)^2}$ and the pole on the line $(\v x-\v z)^2=(\v x-\v w)^2$ has cancelled (numerically, the kernel takes the values $0.19948358$ and $0.19948353$ at distance $\pm10^{-7}$ from the line), so that in the final form the prescription is no longer visible. (A $\pm i\epsilon$ prescription, which is not the one used here, would add terms $\mp i\pi\,\theta\big((\v x-\v z)^2-(\v x-\v w)^2\big)$, from on-shell scattering of the gluon on the charge, to the separate terms.)

\subsection{Instantaneous terms of Group III}\label{sec:fin_inst}

The instantaneous terms $-\frac{1}{k^+}\delta\delta$ of Rows~III.III, III.V and~III.VI depend on $p^+$ only through the phase, and have no pointwise limit for $\vee\to\infty$. With $s=\v k\cdot(\v z-\v y')$ and the $\v y'$ integral done first, Eq.~\eqref{eq:distrib} gives
\begin{align}\label{eq:instdistr}
\int_0^\infty dp^+\,e^{-i\frac{p^+}{k^+}s}=k^+\Big[\pi\,\delta(s)-i\,\mathcal P\frac1s\Big]\qquad\Big(\text{complex conjugate: }\pi\,\delta(s)+i\,\mathcal P\frac1s\Big)\,,
\end{align}
which yields the terms with $\pi\delta(s)-i\mathcal P\frac1s$ of Eqs.~\eqref{eq:app_III31} and~\eqref{eq:app_III5}. They are finite. With $\v r=\v y'-\v z$, the bracket $\Kv^j(x'-y')\mathcal L(y')-\Kv^j(x'-z)\mathcal L(z)$ vanishes at $\v r\to0$, because $\mathcal L^{c'd'b}(y')\to\mathcal L^{c'd'b}(z)$ (the pieces with $U(\v y')U(\v z)$ and with $U(\v z)U(\v z)$ cancel at $\v y'=\v z$) and $\Kv(x'-y')\to\Kv(x'-z)$, so that $\Kv^m(r)[\dots]$ is bounded there. Integrating first over the component of $\v r$ perpendicular to $\v k$, symmetrically at large distance, gives a function of the component $r_\parallel$ along $\v k$ that is $O(r_\parallel)$ for the component $m=\parallel$ and finite at $r_\parallel=0$ for $m=\perp$; $\pi\delta(s)$ and $\mathcal P\frac1s$ then act on a continuous function of $r_\parallel$, and the phase $e^{i\v k\cdot\v r}$ makes the $r_\parallel$ integral converge at large distance. The two terms of the bracket must stay together: each alone grows like $\pm2\pi i\frac{k^m}{\v k^2}\ln\vee$, which is the BFKL term. Numerically, with a smooth test function, the $p^+$ integral cut at $\vee$ approaches the distributional value like $k^+/\vee$, and the distributional form reproduces the limit to $7\times10^{-8}$.

\subsection{The integrable form}\label{sec:fin_integrable}

With the pairs of Section~\ref{sec:fin_soft} and the combination~\eqref{eq:Mhat}, every finite term is integrable over all transverse positions in the sense of Section~\ref{sec:fin_conv}, i.e.\ at fixed positions $\v w$, $\v w'$ of the observed gluon; the region in which the observed and the integrated gluon are far from the projectile together is discussed at the end of this section. Row~I.III, which replaces the separate terms $N_1$, $N_2$, $N_{3a}$, $N_{3b}$, becomes (complex conjugate implied)
\begin{align}\label{eq:GIIII}
\frac{d^3N^{\rm I.III}}{d^3k}&=2i\,\mathcal C_{\rm f}\,f^{abd}\int_{w,w'}e^{-ik\cdot(w-w')}\int_{x,y,z,x'}\Bigg\{P^i\,\Kv^j(y-z)\,\mathcal M^{ij}\notag\\&\qquad+\Big[\ell(z,w,x)-\mathcal E\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)\big)\Big]K(y,w,z)\,P\cdot\Kv(x-w)\Bigg\}\notag\\
&\qquad\times\mathcal L^a\Big[U^{de}(x)\,U^{bc}(z)\,\rho^e(x)\,\rho^c(y)-U^{de}(x)\,U^{bc}(y)\,\rho^e(x)\,\rho^c(y)\Big]\,,
\end{align}
with the $\v w$ integral done before the $\v z$ integral, and the combined kernel (all logarithms with positive arguments, no pole on $(\v x-\v z)^2=(\v x-\v w)^2$)
\begin{align}\label{eq:Mij}
\mathcal M^{ij}&=\ln\frac{(x-z)^2}{(x-w)^2}\Bigg\{\frac{\delta^{ij}}{2(z-w)^2}-\frac{(x-w)^i(x-z)^j}{(x-w)^2(x-z)^2}\notag\\
&\qquad+\frac{1}{(z-w)^2\big[(x-w)^2-(x-z)^2\big]}\Big[(x-w)^i(z-w)^j\frac{(x-z)^2}{(x-w)^2}\notag\\
&\qquad+(z-w)^i(x-z)^j\frac{(x-w)^2}{(x-z)^2}-(z-w)^i(x-w)^j-(x-z)^i(z-w)^j\Big]\Bigg\}\notag\\
&\quad+\frac{\mathcal A_0}{(z-w)^2}\Bigg\{(z-w)^i(x-w)^j+(x-z)^i(z-w)^j\notag\\&\qquad-(x-w)\cdot(x-z)\Big[\frac{(z-w)^i(x-z)^j}{(x-z)^2}+\frac{(x-w)^i(z-w)^j}{(x-w)^2}\Big]\Bigg\}\notag\\
&\quad-\frac{1}{2(z-w)^2}\ln\frac{(z-w)^2}{(x-w)^2}\,\frac{(z-w)^i(x-z)^j}{(x-z)^2}\,,\notag\\
\mathcal A_0&=\frac{\arccos\Big(\frac{(x-w)\cdot(z-w)}{|x-w|\,|z-w|}\Big)}{\sqrt{(x-w)^2(x-z)^2-[(x-w)\cdot(x-z)]^2}}\,.
\end{align}
$\mathcal M^{ij}$ behaves like $\ln|\v z-\v w|/|\v z-\v w|$ at $\v z\to\v w$ (the angular mean of the coefficient of $1/(\v z-\v w)^2$ vanishes), the second term of the brace in Eq.~\eqref{eq:GIIII} like $\ln|\v z-\v w|/|\v z-\v w|$; at $\v x\to\v w$ the brace grows like $\ln|\v x-\v w|/|\v x-\v w|$, at $\v z\to\v x$ and $\v z\to\v y$ like $1/|\cdot|$, and at large $z$ Eq.~\eqref{eq:Mhatclaim} holds.

All soft remainders of Rows~I.III, I.V, III.III, III.V and~III.VI are collected in one block, which we give in two equivalent forms, the paired form~\eqref{eq:Sblock} and the kinematically constrained form~\eqref{eq:Sblockkc} at the end of this section. The paired form is
\begin{align}\label{eq:Sblock}
\frac{d^3N^{\rm soft}}{d^3k}&=\mathcal C_{\rm f}\int_{w,w'}e^{-ik\cdot(w-w')}\int_{x,y,x',z}\Bigg\{\notag\\&\qquad-i\,P\cdot\Kv(y-w)\,\big[2C_A+C_B\big]\,\mathfrak F\big[K(x,\cdot,z)\big]\notag\\
&\qquad-P\cdot\Kv(x-w)\,C_C\,\mathfrak F\big[K(x,\cdot,z)-2K(w,\cdot,z)\big]\notag\\&\qquad+2i\,P\cdot\Kv(x-w)\,C_D\,\mathfrak F\big[K(y,\cdot,z)\big]\Bigg\}\,,\notag\\
\mathfrak F[\kappa]&\equiv\kappa(w)\,\mathcal E\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)\big)-\kappa(w')\,\Psi\big(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w')\big)\,,
\end{align}
where $\kappa(\cdot)$ is evaluated at $\v w$ for the Group~I member and at $\v w'$ for the Group~III member, and $\mathfrak F$ is decomposed as in Eq.~\eqref{eq:rmv}. Both terms of $\mathfrak F$ are integrable; at the coincidence points, $\mathcal E=O(|\v z-\v w|)$ at $\v z\to\v w$ against at most $(\v z-\v w)^{-2}$, $\Psi\to0$ at $\v z\to\v w'$ against $\kappa(w')\sim|\v z-\v w'|^{-1}$, and the singularities at $\v z\to\v x,\v y$ are $|\cdot|^{-1}$. The remaining terms of Rows~I.V, III.III, III.V and~III.VI, the regular terms of Eq.~\eqref{eq:softsplit}, the terms with $\widehat{\mathcal E}$ and the instantaneous terms, are integrable by themselves; they are listed, together with the finite terms of Rows~I.II, II.I, II.IV and~III.II, in Appendix~\ref{app:finite}. Eqs.~\eqref{eq:GIIII}, \eqref{eq:Sblock} and the corresponding equations of Appendix~\ref{app:finite} are exactly the finite terms of the five rows with recoil.

\paragraph{Conditions.} The integrable form holds under the following conditions.
\begin{itemize}
\item[(C1)] $\Lambda\to0$ and $\vee\to\infty$ are taken after the subtractions~\eqref{eq:softsplit}; terms $O(\Lambda/k^+)$ and $O(k^+/\vee)$ are dropped, and the terms $\ln(1-k^+/\vee)$ go with the JIMWLK structures of their rows.
\item[(C2)] The pair identities~\eqref{eq:colid} use only a relabelling of colour indices and $U(\v z)U^T(\v z)=1$; they are operator identities, valid for any $U$. The statements about the $\v z$ integrand hold at fixed values of all other coordinates after the relabellings of integration variables listed in Table~\ref{tab:pairs}.
\item[(C3)] In Eq.~\eqref{eq:GIIII} the $\v w$ integral, i.e.\ the Fourier transform at the measured momentum, is done before the $\v z$ integral. Pointwise in $\v w$ the brace has a $1/\v z^2$ tail; it is absent after the $\v w$ integration, in every direction, Eq.~\eqref{eq:sawid}.
\item[(C4)] Terms $\propto e^{-i\v k\cdot\v z}/\v z^2$ (from the end point $t\to1$ of $\mathcal E$, and from the regions in which the observed gluon is at $\v z$) are treated as convergent Fourier integrals.
\item[(C5)] No assumption on $U(\v z)$ at large distance is made; unitarity of the adjoint Wilson lines is used.
\item[(C6)] The energy denominators of $\mf B_3$ are principal values (Section~\ref{sec:fin_pv}).
\item[(C7)] In the instantaneous terms of Group~III the $\v y'$ integral is done before the $p^+$ integral, the component of $\v y'-\v z$ perpendicular to $\v k$ first and symmetrically at large distance (Section~\ref{sec:fin_inst}).
\item[(C8)] The large-$z$ form~\eqref{eq:saw} of the 24-term kernel is established numerically; its Fourier identity~\eqref{eq:sawid} is analytic.
\item[(C9)] The cancellations were verified on a projectile of charges at two sites (all assignments of the charge positions to the sites, including the contact terms of the three-$\rho$ products), with random classical $\rho$ for the three-$\rho$ part, for a general two-$\rho$ coefficient matrix and for its colour-singlet projection.
\item[(C10)] In the region in which both final-state gluons are far from the projectile together ($\v w,\v w',\v z\to\infty$ at fixed $\v w-\v z$), the finite terms of Row~II.IV, among them the remainder $N_{\mathcal R}$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:dg_R} and the tensor term of Eq.~\eqref{eq:app_IVfin}, have the large-distance tail of Section~\ref{sec:dg_tail}. It cancels at the level of the amplitudes against the same region of the second part of Row~II.I and of Row~III.III, which therefore have to be integrated together with Row~II.IV under one integral over the position of the pair. The finite terms of the second part of Row~II.I and of Row~III.III as written here carry the compensating tail in this joint limit, $1\!:\!1\!:\!-2$ (Section~\ref{sec:dg_tail}, Table~\ref{tab:tails}). For a coloured projectile and a target that is not uniform at large distance a remainder proportional to the total colour charge of the projectile is left; it is the non-singlet term of the JIMWLK formula and is absent for a colour-neutral projectile.
\end{itemize}
The checks are exact identities, pointwise tails in all directions and Fourier integrals at fixed values of the other coordinates; a complete numerical integration of the finite terms over all transverse positions with Wilson lines has not been carried out.

\paragraph{Kinematically constrained form.} The paired form~\eqref{eq:Sblock} leaves the JIMWLK term unchanged. Equivalently, the soft remainders can be combined with the $\ln(k^+/\Lambda)$ terms of the same pairs, $L\,\alpha_XV_XC_X[\kappa_X(w)-\kappa_X(w')]$, which are the contribution of these rows to the JIMWLK term (soft gluon attached to the observed gluon at $\v w$ minus at $\v w'$, a real-minus-virtual combination falling like $|\v z|^{-3}$, with angular mean falling like $|\v z|^{-4}$). For each pair, with $L=\ln(k^+/\Lambda)$ and Eqs.~\eqref{eq:recoilfns},
\begin{align}\label{eq:kinconstr}
\mathfrak F_\Lambda[\kappa]&\equiv\kappa(w)\int_\Lambda^{k^+}\frac{dp^+}{p^+}\,e^{-i\frac{p^+}{k^+}\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)}-\kappa(w')\int_\Lambda^\infty\frac{k^+dp^+}{p^+(p^++k^+)}\,e^{-i\frac{p^+}{k^+}\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w')}\notag\\
&=L\big[\kappa(w)-\kappa(w')\big]+\mathfrak F[\kappa]+O\Big(\frac{\Lambda}{k^+}\Big)\,,
\end{align}
since $\int_\lambda^1\frac{dt}{t}e^{-ist}=L+\mathcal E(s)+O(\lambda)$ and $\int_\lambda^\infty\frac{dt}{t(1+t)}e^{-ist}=L+\Psi(s)+O(\lambda)$, $\lambda=\Lambda/k^+$. The soft-recoil block in kinematically constrained form is the block~\eqref{eq:Sblock} with $\mathfrak F\to\mathfrak F_\Lambda$ (complex conjugate implied),
\begin{align}\label{eq:Sblockkc}
\frac{d^3N^{\rm soft}_{\rm kc}}{d^3k}&=\mathcal C_{\rm f}\int_{w,w'}e^{-ik\cdot(w-w')}\int_{x,y,x',z}\Bigg\{-i\,P\cdot\Kv(y-w)\,\big[2C_A+C_B\big]\,\mathfrak F_\Lambda\big[K(x,\cdot,z)\big]\notag\\
&\qquad-P\cdot\Kv(x-w)\,C_C\,\mathfrak F_\Lambda\big[K(x,\cdot,z)-2K(w,\cdot,z)\big]\notag\\&\qquad+2i\,P\cdot\Kv(x-w)\,C_D\,\mathfrak F_\Lambda\big[K(y,\cdot,z)\big]\Bigg\}\,,
\end{align}
and, by Eq.~\eqref{eq:kinconstr}, the two forms are related by
\begin{align}\label{eq:kcequiv}
\frac{d^3N^{\rm soft}_{\rm kc}}{d^3k}=\frac{d^3N^{\rm soft}}{d^3k}+L\,\frac{d^3N^{\rm J}_{\mathcal S}}{d^3k}+O\Big(\frac{\Lambda}{k^+}\Big)\,,
\end{align}
where $d^3N^{\rm J}_{\mathcal S}/d^3k$ is the block~\eqref{eq:Sblock} with $\mathfrak F[\kappa]\to\kappa(w)-\kappa(w')$, i.e.\ $L\,d^3N^{\rm J}_{\mathcal S}/d^3k$ is the part of the JIMWLK term that comes from the pairs A--D. In the paired form the JIMWLK term is kept whole and the finite block is $N^{\rm soft}$; in the kinematically constrained form $L\,N^{\rm J}_{\mathcal S}$ is taken out of the JIMWLK term and $N^{\rm soft}_{\rm kc}$ is used in place of $N^{\rm soft}$. The sum is the same, $N^{\rm JIMWLK}+N^{\rm soft}=\big[N^{\rm JIMWLK}-L\,N^{\rm J}_{\mathcal S}\big]+N^{\rm soft}_{\rm kc}$ up to $O(\Lambda/k^+)$; all other finite terms, in particular Eq.~\eqref{eq:GIIII} with its term $(\ell-\mathcal E)$, are the same in both forms. $N^{\rm soft}_{\rm kc}$ is integrable at large $\v z$, being the sum of the integrable $L\,N^{\rm J}_{\mathcal S}$ and $N^{\rm soft}$. In this form each kernel of the JIMWLK term that carries a recoil phase comes with its own rapidity interval, $\simeq\ln\frac{k^+}{\Lambda|\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)|}-\gamma_E-\frac{i\pi}{2}{\rm sgn}(\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w))$ for $|\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)|\gg1$ (resp.\ with $\v w'$) and $\ln\frac{k^+}{\Lambda}$ for $|\v k\cdot(\v z-\v w)|\ll1$: the soft gluon has a rapidity below that of the observed gluon, as in the kinematically constrained formulations of forward production~\cite{Altinoluk:2014eka,Ducloue:2016shw} (the constraint of~\cite{Ducloue:2016shw} has not been compared in detail). Also the virtual (Group~I) members of the pairs get a $\v z$-dependent interval, while the terms of the JIMWLK term that carry no recoil phase keep $\ln\frac{k^+}{\Lambda}$. \pending{For pair~D the Group~I member is the part $\mathcal E$ of the function $\ell$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:ell}, which has no $\ln(k^+/\Lambda)$ term of its own in Row~I.III (Section~\ref{sec:fin_GIIII}); that $L\,\alpha_DV_DC_D[\kappa_D(w)-\kappa_D(w')]$ is part of the JIMWLK term is taken from the fix note (fix\_soft, Step~7) and has not been re-derived here. The identity~\eqref{eq:kcequiv} does not depend on it.}

\subsection{Average over the projectile and colour-singlet form}\label{sec:fin_singlet}

\paragraph{Assumptions.} Every finite term contains an ordered product of two or three charges, $C^{a_1\dots a_n}\rho^{a_1}(p_1)\cdots\rho^{a_n}(p_n)$, with a $c$-number tensor $C$ built from adjoint Wilson lines, $f^{abc}$ and $N_c$. We assume
\begin{itemize}
\item[(A1)] \emph{Global colour invariance:} the projectile density matrix commutes with the total charge $Q^a=\int_x\rho^a(\v x)$. Then $\langle\rho^a(\v x)\rangle=0$, and the correlator is colour diagonal, Eq.~\eqref{eq:singlet}, with $n(\v x,\v y)=n(\v y,\v x)=n(\v x,\v y)^*$; the symmetry follows from $\langle[\rho^a(\v x),\rho^b(\v y)]\rangle=0$.
\item[(A2)] \emph{No symmetrised three-point function:} $S^{abc}(\v x,\v y,\v z)\equiv\langle\{\rho^a(\v x)\rho^b(\v y)\rho^c(\v z)\}_{\rm sym}\rangle=0$, where $\{\dots\}_{\rm sym}$ is the average over the six orderings.
\end{itemize}
(A2) holds for a Gaussian projectile (Gaussian Weyl symbol of the density matrix, as in the McLerran--Venugopalan model): the symmetrised products are the classical moments of the Weyl symbol, and its odd moments vanish. In general, by (A1), $S^{abc}=d^{abc}g_d+f^{abc}g_f$ with $g_d$ totally symmetric and $g_f$ totally antisymmetric in the three points; $g_d$ is C-odd (odderon-like), and C-evenness alone does not remove $g_f$. Nothing else is assumed; in particular no assumption on the four-point function is needed, since the four-$\rho$ rows contribute only to the evolution terms.

\paragraph{Quadratic and cubic terms.} By (A1) the order of two charges does not matter, $\langle C^{ab}\rho^a(p)\rho^b(q)\rangle=C^{aa}\,n(p,q)/(N_c^2-1)$. For three charges, reordering the product into the symmetrised one produces single commutators, which are \emph{two}-$\rho$ operators, and double commutators, which are one-$\rho$ operators with zero average. With $\Phi\equiv C^{abc}f^{abc}$, the Wilson lines evaluated at the coincident points where the $\delta$-function puts them,
\begin{align}\label{eq:cubicavg}
\langle C^{abc}\rho^a(p_1)\rho^b(p_2)\rho^c(p_3)\rangle&=C^{abc}S^{abc}+\frac{i\Phi}{2(N_c^2-1)}\Big[\delta^{(2)}(p_1-p_2)\,n(p_1,p_3)\notag\\&\quad+\delta^{(2)}(p_2-p_3)\,n(p_1,p_2)-\delta^{(2)}(p_1-p_3)\,n(p_1,p_2)\Big]\,,\notag\\
\langle C^{abc}\rho^a(p_1)\{\rho^b(p_2),\rho^c(p_3)\}\rangle&=2C^{abc}S^{abc}\notag\\&\quad+\frac{i\Phi}{N_c^2-1}\Big[\delta^{(2)}(p_1-p_2)\,n(p_1,p_3)-\delta^{(2)}(p_1-p_3)\,n(p_1,p_2)\Big]\,,\notag\\
\langle C^{abc}\{\rho^a(p_1),\rho^b(p_2)\}\rho^c(p_3)\rangle&=2C^{abc}S^{abc}\notag\\&\quad+\frac{i\Phi}{N_c^2-1}\Big[\delta^{(2)}(p_2-p_3)\,n(p_1,p_2)-\delta^{(2)}(p_1-p_3)\,n(p_1,p_2)\Big]\,.
\end{align}
Under (A2) the first term vanishes, \emph{but the contact terms do not}: they are two-point terms $\propto\delta^{(2)}(p_i-p_j)\,n$, of the same order as the quadratic terms, and they are not removed by colour neutrality. \emph{The cubic terms therefore do not vanish after the average over a Gaussian projectile; only their symmetrised part does.} The contact terms are written below as quadratic terms at coincident points, $\mathfrak K|_{q\to p}$ denoting the kernel with the point $q$ replaced by $p$.

\paragraph{Parity in $\v k$.} Write a term as $A(\v k)\langle O\rangle$, with $A$ the $c$-number factor and $O$ the colour operator. All kernels depend on $\v k$ through functions with $f(-s)=f(s)^*$ ($e^{-i\v k\cdot\v r}$, $\mathcal E$, $\mathcal J$, $\widehat{\mathcal E}$, $\Psi$, $\pi\delta(s)-i\mathcal P\frac1s$); the quadratic rows have real prefactors and the cubic rows an explicit $i$ (in Row~I.II, $N_{2b}$, the $i$ is in the Fourier transform of the odd function $\mathcal R^i$). Hence $A(-\v k)=A(\v k)^*$ for the quadratic and $A(-\v k)=-A(\v k)^*$ for the cubic rows; for the first part of Row~II.I, which has no complex conjugate, the same holds for the sum $N_{1a}+N_{1b}$. With $\langle O\rangle=\mathsf S+i\mathsf C$, $\mathsf S=C^{abc}S^{abc}$ and the contact part $\mathsf C$ real, a cubic term plus its complex conjugate is $\mathsf S[A(\v k)-A(-\v k)]+i\mathsf C[A(\v k)+A(-\v k)]$. The symmetrised part of the cubic terms is odd under $\v k\to-\v k$, their contact part is even, and the quadratic terms are even. The part of the finite terms that is odd in $\v k$ is exactly the $S^{abc}$ part; it requires a C-odd ($d^{abc}$) or a $g_f$ three-point function and vanishes under (A2). This refines the statement that the cubic, ``first saturation'' terms are odd in $\v k$~\cite{McLerran:2016snu,Kovchegov:2018jun}: it holds for their symmetrised part, configuration by configuration, but not for the commutator remainders of the ordered products.

\paragraph{Colour-singlet structures.} With adjoint $U$ (real, $U^TU=1$), $(T^e)_{bc}=-if^{ebc}$, ${\rm Tr}\,T^aT^b=N_c\delta^{ab}$, we define
\begin{align}\label{eq:DFQ}
\mf D(a,b)&\equiv{\rm Tr}\{U^\dagger(a)U(b)\}\,,\notag\\
\mf F(a,b,c)&\equiv{\rm Tr}\{T^eU^\dagger(a)U(b)T^eU^\dagger(b)U(c)\}=f^{ijk}f^{lmn}\,U^{il}(a)\,U^{jm}(b)\,U^{kn}(c)\,,\notag\\
\mf Q(a,b;c,d)&\equiv{\rm Tr}\{T^eU^\dagger(a)U(b)T^eU^\dagger(c)U(d)\}\,.
\end{align}

The Wilson-line arguments are ordered by the fixed list $x,y,x',y',z,w,w'$. All singlet factors below were obtained by machine from the colour structures of Appendix~\ref{app:finite} and verified numerically: the colour algebra with explicit $SU(2)$ and $SU(3)$ generators and random Wilson lines (to $9\times10^{-15}$), and at the operator level with charges represented by generators on two or three lattice sites and globally colour-invariant density matrices (adjoint and fundamental charges, C-even and C-odd ensembles, with and without a chiral $f^{abc}\rho\rho\rho$ term; largest relative deviation $6\times10^{-15}$). In the ensembles in which (A2) is violated the $S^{abc}$ part of every cubic structure was found to be real, which with $A(-\v k)=-A(\v k)^*$ confirms that it is odd in $\v k$.

\paragraph{The pairs in colour-singlet form.} Since the identities~\eqref{eq:colid} are operator identities, the pair cancellation of Section~\ref{sec:fin_soft} holds line by line in the colour-singlet form: after the relabellings of Table~\ref{tab:pairs} the line of the Group~III member and the line of the Group~I member with the same contact have equal and opposite colour factors (for instance, at $\v y'=\v w'$ the factor of the line $\mathfrak K_\Psi|_{x'\to x}$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:sIIIV} is minus that of the line $\mathfrak K^{\rm III}_w|_{x'\to x}$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:sIIII}), and the two lines combine into $\mathfrak F[\kappa]$. The integrable grouping is therefore: pair~A, the lines with $\mathfrak K^{\rm III}_w$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:sIIII} with the lines $\mathfrak K_\Psi$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:sIIIV}; pair~B, the terms with $\mathcal E$ of $\mathfrak K^{\rm V}_1$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:sIV1} with the lines $\mathfrak K_\Psi$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:sIIIIII1}; pair~C, the recoil terms of $\mathcal F^{im}$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:sIV2} with the term $\mathfrak K_{\Psi,2}$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:sIIIIII2}; pair~D, the part $\mathcal E$ of $\ell$ in the lines with $\mathfrak K^{\rm III}_z$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:sIIII} with the lines $\mathfrak K_\Psi$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:sIIIVI}; the rest of $\mathfrak K^{\rm III}_z$ is $\widehat{\mathcal M}$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:Mhat}, integrated over $\v w$ first. The tail cancellation of the pairs was verified numerically also on the singlet projection. The contact points are $\rho$ points, never the soft position $\v z$; hence, after $U^TU=1$, the terms of the singlet factors that contain $\v z$ are the part linear in $U(\v z)$ and the others the $\v z$-independent part.

\paragraph{Remarks.} (i) The contact terms of the cubic rows are part of the result for a Gaussian, colour-neutral projectile. Whether they cancel among the rows, or against the corresponding contact terms of the three-$\rho$ evolution terms, has not been examined; this would require the kernels to be integrated, and the kernels differ from row to row. (ii) \pending{The integrability of the contact kernels has been checked explicitly only for $\mathfrak K^{\rm III}_z|_{y\to x}$ and $\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}|_{y\to x}$ at the new coincidence $\v x\to\v z$, where they grow like $\ln(1/r)/r$, i.e.\ integrably. No kernel depends on the difference of two charge positions that are merged, so no kernel is singular at the contact itself, but the new coincidences of the merged point with $\v z$, $\v w$, $\v w'$, $\v y'$ have not been power-counted row by row.} (iii) A C-odd ($d^{abc}$) or chiral ($f^{abc}g_f$) three-point function of the projectile would add the $S^{abc}$ parts, odd in $\v k$.

\subsection{Summary of the finite terms}\label{sec:fin_summary}

The finite terms are given by Eqs.~\eqref{eq:GIIII} and~\eqref{eq:Sblock} and by the equations of Appendix~\ref{app:finite}, for an arbitrary ordered charge correlator, and by Eqs.~\eqref{eq:sIIa}--\eqref{eq:sIIIVI} and~\eqref{eq:sLOrepl} in colour-singlet form. They contain no renormalisation scale and no rapidity cut-off, and they are integrable at fixed positions of the observed gluon under the conditions (C1)--(C9); for the region of a distant pair of final-state gluons see (C10). The double logarithms $\ln^2(k^+/\Lambda)$ of the individual rows cancel in the sum; the soft remainders of the plus prescriptions cancel in four pairs, each pair consisting of a virtual (Group~I) and a real (Group~III) term with the same coefficient, colour structure and kernel and with the recoil on $\v w$ and on $\v w'$, respectively, without any condition on the target at large distance. The third term of $N_{3b}$ of Row~I.III, whose $\ln[(\v z-\v w)^2/(\v x-\v w)^2]$ made the row non-integrable, is cancelled by the soft remainder of Row~III.VI together with the odd-angle tail of the 24 other terms of its own row, after the Fourier integration over the position of the observed gluon. After the average over a Gaussian, colour-singlet projectile the finite terms are even in $\v k$; the three-$\rho$ rows contribute through the contact terms of the commutators of the ordered charges. In addition, the remainder $N_{\mathcal R}$ of the local form of the collinear terms, Eq.~\eqref{eq:dg_R}, and its large-distance tail belong to the finite terms of Row~II.IV and have to be integrated together with the second part of Row~II.I and with Row~III.III (Section~\ref{sec:dg_tail}); the term-by-term check of this cancellation on the finite terms as written is still open, (C10).
