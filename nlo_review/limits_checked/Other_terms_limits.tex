Conventions: phase $e^{-ik\cdot(w-w')}$; c.c.\ implied for Groups I and III; principal value, logs are $\log|\dots|$; plus prescription $\int dp^{+}\,[1/p^{+}]_{+}f(p^{+})=\int dp^{+}\,(f(p^{+})-f(0))/p^{+}$; one scale $\mu$; the running-coupling term is the $\delta(1-\xi)$ part of DGLAP. \section{Group I (with c.c.)}
\noindent Limits taken here: $\Lambda\to0$ and $\vee\to\infty$ wherever the limit exists at fixed transverse coordinates \emph{and} the limiting integrand is integrable where the original was. Marking: {\color{red}red} = changed with respect to \texttt{Other\_terms}; {\color{orange}orange} = dropped by the limit; {\color{magenta}magenta} = flag (limit cannot be taken, the term is kept with $\Lambda$ and/or $\vee$ explicit). Each equation carries a category tag: \textbf{[UV]}, \textbf{[RC]}, \textbf{[DGLAP]}, \textbf{[finite]}; the categories are collected in Sec.~``Classification'' at the end of this section. All logarithms are $\log|\dots|$. Shorthands used below (only to shorten the lines):
\begin{align}
\mathcal{L}^{a}_{x'w'} &\equiv U^{ab'}(x')\,\rho^{b'}(x')-U^{ab'}(w')\,\rho^{b'}(x')\,,\notag\\
\mathcal{C}_{\rm III} &\equiv \mathcal{L}^{a}_{x'w'}\times\left[U^{de}(x)\,U^{bc}(z)\,\rho^{e}(x)\,\rho^{c}(y)-U^{de}(x)\,U^{bc}(y)\,\rho^{e}(x)\,\rho^{c}(y)\right]\,,\notag\\
\mathcal{C}_{\rm IV} &\equiv \mathcal{L}^{a}_{x'w'}\left[f^{cdc'}\,U^{ac}(w)\,U^{bd}(z)\,U^{be}(y)\,\rho^{e}(y)\rho^{c'}(x)-f^{abc}\,U^{be}(y)\,U^{cd}(x)\,\rho^{e}(y)\rho^{d}(x)\right]\,.\notag
\end{align}
General remark on large distances (not a flag): the $w$ integrals converge only through the phase $e^{-ik\cdot w}$, as at LO (kernels $\sim1/|w|$). Where a transverse logarithm was cut off by $k^{+}/\vee$, the limit $\vee\to\infty$ changes the large-$w$ behaviour from $1/|w|^{n}$ to $\log(w^{2})/|w|^{n}$ in the region $(x-w)^{2}>\frac{\vee}{k^{+}}(x-z)^{2}$; this does not spoil the (conditional) convergence: e.g. for the kernel $(x-w)^{i}/(x-w)^{2}$ the difference between finite $\vee$ and the limit is $\propto\int_{0}^{\infty}dt\,J_{1}(t)\log\big|1-\frac{t^{2}}{s^{2}}\big|=-\pi Y_{0}(s)$, $s=|k|\,|x-z|\sqrt{\vee/k^{+}}$, i.e. it vanishes like $(k^{+}/\vee)^{1/4}$ (checked numerically).

No pole multiplies it: it is $O(1/|z-w|)$ at $z\to w$, decays like $\frac{p^{+}}{|z|\,(p^{+}z^{2}+k^{+}(x-w)^{2})}$ at large $z$ and like $1/p^{+\,2}$ at large $p^{+}$; $d_{\perp}\to2$.
\subsubsection{(ii) Running-coupling terms}
\noindent None.
\subsubsection{(iii) DGLAP terms}
\noindent None. With $\xi=\frac{k^{+}}{p^{+}+k^{+}}$ the kernels of Rows III, V, VI are $\frac{dp^{+}}{p^{+}+k^{+}}=\frac{d\xi}{\xi}$ and $\frac{k^{+}dp^{+}}{p^{+}(p^{+}+k^{+})}=\frac{d\xi}{1-\xi}$, i.e. the end-point parts $\frac{1-\xi}{\xi}$ and $\frac{\xi}{(1-\xi)_{+}}$ of the splitting function, but these terms have three charge densities, no factor $\mathcal P_{gg}(\xi)$ and no collinear logarithm $\log\frac{r^{2}\mu^{2}}{4\xi^{2}}$; they are not of DGLAP type.
\subsubsection{(iv) Finite terms}
\noindent Rows I, II: finite, limits taken. Rows III, V, VI: finite at fixed transverse coordinates, with FLAG III-1 ($\Lambda\to0$, large $z$), FLAG III-2 ($\vee\to\infty$ only after the transverse integration) and FLAG III-3.
